# SECTION 2: BRAND VOICE GUIDE

*Choose the voice template below that best matches your property and target guest. Mix elements from multiple templates if needed.*

## Voice Template 1: Luxury / Premium

**Best for:** High-ADR properties ($500+/night), wine country, upscale lakefront/beachfront, designer interiors, adults-focused.

**Tone:** Sophisticated, aspirational, understated confidence. You are not selling a rental — you are offering an experience that cannot be replicated.

**Words to use:** Curated, bespoke, retreat, indulge, elevated, estate, reserve, private, signature, artisan, handcrafted, destination, exclusive, refined.

**Words to avoid:** Cheap, deal, budget, crash, party house, affordable, basic.

**Color Palette for Graphics:**
- Primary: Deep navy (#1B2A4A) or forest green (#2C3E2D)
- Secondary: Warm gold (#C9A96E) or champagne (#F5E6CC)
- Accent: Cream (#FAF8F5) or soft white
- Text: Charcoal (#2D2D2D) on light, cream on dark
- Fonts: Serif headers (Playfair Display, Cormorant), clean sans-serif body (Montserrat, Lato)

**Photography Style:**
- Shoot during golden hour (sunrise/sunset) exclusively for hero shots
- Stage with fresh flowers, wine glasses, linen napkins, charcuterie boards
- Use negative space — do not overcrowd the frame
- Show people from behind or at a distance (silhouettes at sunset, couple on the dock)
- Edit with warm, muted tones — no oversaturation
- Always include one lifestyle element (book on the nightstand, wine by the fire, towel rolled on lounger)

**Caption Voice:** Third person, editorial. Write like a boutique hotel's Instagram.

**Example caption:**
> Sunset settles over Seneca Lake as the evening unfolds exactly as it should — unhurried, unfiltered, unforgettable. Five bedrooms. Two hundred feet of private waterfront. One address worth remembering. Link in bio.

---

## Voice Template 2: Family / Welcoming

**Best for:** 3+ bedroom family properties, kid-friendly amenities, practical layouts, moderate ADR ($200-$600/night), repeat family bookings.

**Tone:** Warm, genuine, helpful, reassuring. You are the friend who always knows the best local spots and makes every family feel at home.

**Words to use:** Gather, memories, together, spacious, home away from home, explore, discover, easy, kid-friendly, perfect for families, cozy, welcoming.

**Words to avoid:** Exclusive, luxury (unless it genuinely applies), VIP, elite, adults-only.

**Color Palette for Graphics:**
- Primary: Warm blue (#4A90B8) or sage green (#87A878)
- Secondary: Sunshine yellow (#F2C94C) or coral (#E8836B)
- Accent: Soft white (#FFFFFF) or light cream
- Text: Dark gray (#333333) on light backgrounds
- Fonts: Rounded sans-serif headers (Nunito, Quicksand), friendly body (Open Sans, Source Sans Pro)

**Photography Style:**
- Capture real moments — kids running on the lawn, family around the fire pit, board games on the table
- Bright, natural lighting — airy and cheerful
- Show the practical: the bunk room setup, the stocked kitchen, the fenced yard
- Include "parent magnets": coffee station close-up, quiet reading nook, master suite with door that locks
- Shoot the nearby kid-friendly attractions (mini golf, ice cream shop, splash pad)

**Caption Voice:** First person, conversational. Write like you are texting a friend who asked "where should we take the kids this summer?"

**Example caption:**
> Real talk: the best part of vacation is when the kids are OUT and the adults are sitting by the fire with a glass of wine wondering why they waited so long to book. Our place sleeps 12 and has enough space that everyone gets their own corner. Summer weekends are filling up — grab yours. Link in bio.

## Voice Template 3: Adventure / Experiential

**Best for:** Mountain cabins, outdoor-access properties, lake properties with water sports, rural retreats near hiking/biking, active travelers, group trips.

**Tone:** Energetic, inspiring, bold, FOMO-inducing. You are not renting a house — you are unlocking access to the best version of the outdoors.

**Words to use:** Epic, explore, adventure awaits, your basecamp, trailhead, unlock, wild, discover, bucket list, off the beaten path, sunrise, summit.

**Words to avoid:** Relaxing (unless pairing with adventure), quiet getaway, sleepy, quaint.

**Color Palette for Graphics:**
- Primary: Burnt orange (#D4722A) or deep teal (#1A6B6A)
- Secondary: Charcoal (#2D2D2D) or slate (#4A5568)
- Accent: Golden yellow (#F0B429) or forest green (#2D6A4F)
- Text: White on dark overlay, charcoal on light
- Fonts: Bold condensed headers (Oswald, Barlow Condensed), clean body (Inter, Rubik)

**Photography Style:**
- Action and movement — kayaks on the water, feet on the trail, jumping off the dock
- Wide-angle landscape shots with the property in context (show the mountain, the lake, the forest)
- Drone shots showing proximity to nature
- Sunrise and blue-hour shots for dramatic sky
- Show gear: bikes on the porch, fishing rods by the door, ski boots by the fire
- Edit with contrast and vibrancy — bold, punchy colors

**Caption Voice:** Second person, direct. Write like a friend daring you to say yes.

**Example caption:**
> Your morning commute: 14 steps from the bedroom to the dock. Your office: a kayak in the middle of Seneca Lake. Your excuse for not booking: we are waiting. 5 beds, 200 ft of private waterfront, and the Finger Lakes Wine Trail starts at the end of the driveway. Link in bio.
