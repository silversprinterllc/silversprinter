### Email body

Hey {{first_name}},

Here's the uncomfortable truth:

You can have the most beautiful direct booking site on the internet, and it doesn't matter if nobody knows it exists.

The fastest way to make it matter: capture the email of every guest who has *already stayed with you*.

Your past Airbnb guests are a goldmine. They've already vetted you. They already had a great stay. They're 5-7x more likely to book direct next time than a cold visitor from Google — IF you can reach them.

Here's how I do it at Lakeside Landing. Three layers:

**Layer 1: StayFi (or equivalent WiFi capture)**

StayFi is ~$15/property/month. It replaces your guest WiFi splash page. Before guests connect, they enter their real email + first name. You own that list forever.

Last year I captured 287 guest emails this way off 63 bookings (guests, not bookings — avg party size 4.5). Airbnb gave me... zero.

Alternative if you don't want StayFi: Eero with captive portal, or Plume.

**Layer 2: Pre-arrival guest form**

In your Airbnb/VRBO message flow, send a "guest info form" 3 days before arrival. Frame it as: *"So we can personalize your stay — gate code, WiFi, wineries picked for your group."*

Use Tally, Typeform, or an OwnerRez embedded form. Ask for: name, email, phone, arrival time, any celebrations, dietary notes. Every single field is a reason to email them later ("It's your anniversary month — come back for it").

Airbnb allows this. They don't allow you to *advertise* a direct booking link inside their platform until after checkout. Big difference. Know the rule.

**Layer 3: Departure follow-up**

48 hours after checkout, a simple personal email: *"Hope the drive home was easy. Quick favor — if you had a great time, a review on Airbnb would mean the world. And if you're thinking about next year, here's our direct rate: [link]."*

That's it. That one email, last year, drove 7 direct rebookings at Lakeside Landing. Seven. At $1,100 ADR × 3-night average = ~$23,100 in revenue that Airbnb took $0 fee on.

**Your action for today:**

Pick ONE of the three layers and set it up this weekend. Just one. StayFi is the fastest (45 min). Pre-arrival form is the cheapest (free). Either works.

Tomorrow: the exact 5 email sequences I run on those guests once they're on the list.

*"If you know anyone who'd love Lakeside, here's $150 off for them — and $150 off your next stay for you."* Share-link style. One email, 3-4 sends per year. Referrals are my highest-ADR, lowest-hassle guests.

**5. Review sequence** (split into Airbnb review ask + Google review ask, 24 hrs apart)

Airbnb ask first (protects your platform rank). Google ask second (builds SEO for direct searches). Most hosts only do one or the other. Do both.

## Recommended Email Platform: **Kit (formerly ConvertKit)**

**Why Kit for this sequence:**
- Tag-based automation beats list-based for this use case — one subscriber can hold multiple tags (quiz_score_high, clicked_course, past_buyer) without duplicate records
- Visual automation builder — you can map the 7-day course + cart sequence as one flow
- Native integration with Typeform, Tally, most quiz tools, and Stripe
- Free tier up to 10k subscribers (switch to paid once list grows — typically $49/mo at 5k)
- Built-in broadcast editor for future one-off emails outside the course

**Alternatives considered:**
- **MailerLite** — Cheaper, equally capable for this flow. Recommend if budget is primary concern.
- **Klaviyo** — Overkill unless you're doing advanced segmentation with product catalogs. Built for e-commerce.
- **ActiveCampaign** — More powerful but steeper curve. Recommend only if you already know it.
- **Mailchimp** — Skip. The free tier has stripped-down automations since 2019.

## Tagging Strategy

Keep tags to a small, clean taxonomy. Too many tags = unusable list.

**Source tags** (how they got in): `source_quiz`, `source_calculator`, `source_guide`, `source_webinar`, `source_podcast`, `source_referral`

**Interest tags** (what they clicked): `interest_pricing`, `interest_direct_booking`, `interest_email`, `interest_creators`

**Qualification tags** (quiz scoring):
- `quiz_score_low` — 0-40 pts — beginner, high-touch nurture before course offer
- `quiz_score_mid` — 41-70 pts — ideal course buyer, default sequence
- `quiz_score_high` — 71-100 pts — experienced host, may convert to DFY bundle instead

**Behavioral tags:** `opened_50pct`, `clicked_course`, `course_purchased`, `refunded`, `cold_90d` (auto-apply if no open in 90 days — cleanup target)

**Segmentation example:**
- Want to pitch the $25K DFY bundle? Segment: `quiz_score_high` AND `clicked_course` AND NOT `course_purchased` AND opened > 70% of emails.
