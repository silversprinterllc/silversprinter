# LESSON 1: The Creator Economy for STR (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"A travel creator with 15,000 followers posts a 30-second Reel of your property. It gets 50,000 views. 500 people click through to your direct booking site. 15 of them book. Total cost: a 2-night complimentary stay worth $500. That's a $4,000+ return on a $500 investment. This isn't hypothetical — it's what happens when you work with the right creators."

### WHY MICRO > MACRO [SLIDES]
"Forget the Kardashians. The sweet spot for STR is micro-influencers: 5,000 to 50,000 followers.

Why micro beats macro:
- **Engagement rate:** Micro-influencers average 3-5% engagement vs 1-2% for macro. Their audience actually pays attention.
- **Geographic relevance:** A local travel creator's audience is full of people who ACTUALLY visit your area. A celebrity's audience is global — 99% will never come to your city.
- **Cost:** Micro-influencers will often accept a free stay. Macro charges $2,000-$10,000+ per post.
- **Authenticity:** A genuine post from a real traveler converts better than a polished ad from a celebrity.
- **Repeatability:** You can do 4-6 micro-influencer stays per year. You can afford one macro post ever.

**The math per stay:**
- Your cost: 2-3 nights of vacancy (~$400-$750)
- Their deliverables: 2-3 Reels/TikToks, 5-10 Stories, 1 blog post or review
- Expected reach: 20,000-100,000 impressions
- Expected site visits: 200-1,000
- Expected bookings: 5-20 (over the content's lifespan)
- ROI: 5x-20x your cost"

### FINDING THE RIGHT CREATORS [CANON 70D + SLIDES]
"Not all creators are equal. Here's the 5-point vetting checklist:

**1. Engagement Rate (>2%)**
Divide average likes + comments by follower count. If someone has 20,000 followers but gets 50 likes per post, their audience is dead (or fake).

**2. Audience Geography**
Their followers need to be in your feeder markets — the cities guests fly in from. A creator based in your city is ideal. A creator whose audience is primarily in a country that doesn't visit your market is useless.

**3. Content Quality**
Watch their last 10 posts. Is the photography good? Would you be proud to have this content associated with your property? If their content looks amateur, it won't elevate your brand.

**4. Audience Demographics**
Their followers should match your ideal guest: age, income level, travel style. Family travel creator for a family property. Luxury lifestyle for a luxury property. Adventure for adventure markets.

**5. Past Brand Work**
Have they done property or hospitality collaborations before? Check for hotel stays, restaurant reviews, travel brand partnerships. A creator who already does this type of content will produce better results."

### WHERE TO FIND THEM [SLIDES]
"**Instagram:** Search hashtags like #[yourcity]travel, #[yourcity]blogger, #travelblogger[yourcity]. Browse the top posts. Follow the creators who match your vetting criteria.

**TikTok:** Search your city name + 'travel' or 'vacation.' Browse creators who make content about your area.

**YouTube:** Search '[your city] travel guide' or '[your city] vlog.' Smaller channels (5K-50K subscribers) are your targets.

**Creator Marketplaces:** Aspire, Collabstr, The Influence Agency — platforms that connect brands with creators. You can filter by location, audience size, and niche.

**Your own guest list:** Check if any past guests are content creators. They already love your property — easiest outreach ever."

# LESSON 2: Outreach & Deal Structure (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Most creator outreach gets ignored. 'Hey, want a free stay?' doesn't cut it. In this lesson, I'll give you the exact outreach template that gets a 40%+ response rate — and the deal structure that protects you and delivers real results."

### THE OUTREACH TEMPLATE [CANON 70D + SLIDES]
"Here's the DM or email that works. It's direct, specific, and professional:

**Subject/Opening:** 'Collaboration at [Property Name] — Love Your Content'

**Body:**
'Hi [Name],

I've been following your content on [platform] — your [specific post they made] was incredible. The way you captured [specific detail] really stood out.

I own [Property Name], a [X]-bedroom [type] in [City] with [top 2-3 amenities]. I think your audience would love it, and I'd love to host you for a complimentary [2-3] night stay.

In exchange, here's what I'm thinking:
- [2-3] Instagram Reels or TikToks featuring the property and local area
- [5-10] Instagram Stories during your stay
- Content rights for us to repost on our channels

No scripts, no forced angles — just your authentic experience at the property. I'd also love for you to explore [specific local experience or restaurant] during your stay.

Would you be interested? Happy to chat details.

[Your name]
[Property name + link]'

**Why this works:**
- Opens with genuine compliment on SPECIFIC content (proves you actually follow them)
- Clear offer: free stay, specific duration
- Clear deliverables: exact number of posts, Stories
- Mentions content rights (critical — we cover this next)
- Low pressure: 'no scripts, no forced angles'
- Specific local experience suggestion (shows you're curating their stay)"

### DEAL STRUCTURE & CONTENT RIGHTS [SLIDES]
"Before confirming any creator stay, send a simple agreement. Template provided.

**The agreement covers:**
1. **Stay dates and duration** (2-3 nights)
2. **Deliverables** (number and type of posts, Stories, blog mentions)
3. **Posting timeline** (within 7-14 days of the stay)
4. **Content rights:** You get permission to repost their content on your channels and website for 12 months. This is non-negotiable — the content is half the value.
5. **Tagging and linking:** They tag your property account and include your direct booking URL
6. **Cancellation terms:** If they cancel within 7 days, the stay is forfeited
7. **What you provide:** Complimentary stay, local experience recommendations, any specific amenities/staging

Keep it to one page. Don't make it feel like a legal battle — it's a collaboration agreement.

**When to pay instead of trade:**
- If the creator has 30K+ followers with strong engagement: offer stay + $300-$500 stipend
- If you need very specific content (professional video, drone footage): pay for the production upgrade
- If the creator is a professional photographer: their content is worth paying for — you'll use those photos for years"

# LESSON 3: Managing Stays & Building a Network (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"The creator said yes. Now what? The stay itself IS the production. How you set it up determines whether you get forgettable content or a piece that drives bookings for months. Let me show you how to manage the stay, maximize the content, and turn one-off collaborations into an ongoing creator network."

### PRE-STAY PREPARATION [SLIDES]
"Send the Creator Briefing Document 5-7 days before arrival:

1. **Check-in details** (same as any guest)
2. **Property highlights:** Top 3 things to feature (the pool, the view, the outdoor kitchen)
3. **Suggested shot list:** 'Here are angles and moments other creators have loved — but feel free to add your own style'
   - Arrival moment (opening the door for the first time)
   - Morning coffee on the deck
   - Pool/hot tub at golden hour
   - Best sunset viewing spot
   - Kitchen cooking moment
   - The bedroom reveal
4. **Local experiences booked:** 'We've arranged a complimentary [dinner/tour/activity] at [venue] — we think your audience will love it'
5. **WiFi, parking, house rules** (standard guest info)
6. **Your social handles** so they know what to tag
7. **Branded welcome item:** Property-branded tote bag, candle, or coffee mug they can feature and keep"

### DURING THE STAY [CANON 70D]
"Be available but not overbearing. Check in via text on arrival:

'Welcome! Everything looking good? Let me know if you need anything at all — here to help but not hovering. Enjoy and have fun creating!'

Don't ask to see content during the stay. Don't micromanage. Let them do their thing. The best content comes when creators feel natural, not directed.

**One exception:** If they're open to it, offer to film a short video together — host-creator collaboration content performs exceptionally well because it adds personal connection."

### POST-STAY & CONTENT REPURPOSING [SLIDES]
"After the stay:

**Day 1:** Thank-you message: 'Thanks for an amazing stay! Can't wait to see the content. No rush — just tag us when you're ready to post.'

**Day 7-14:** If nothing posted yet, gentle nudge: 'Hey [Name]! Just checking in — do you have a rough timeline for when the posts will go up? Excited to share your perspective with our audience!'

**Once content is posted:**
1. Like, comment, and repost to your Stories immediately
2. Save the content (download Reels/TikToks for your library)
3. Add to your 'Creator Content' folder
4. Repost to your feed within 1-2 weeks (with credit)
5. Use their photos on your direct booking website (per your agreement)
6. Feature in your email newsletter: 'See what [Creator Name] loved about [Property]'
7. Use as Pinterest pins (with credit)

**One creator stay should generate 3-6 months of repurposable content.**"

### BUILDING THE NETWORK [CANON 70D]
"Move from one-off stays to an ongoing program:

**Annual Creator Calendar:** Plan 4-6 creator stays per year (roughly one every 2 months)
- Q1: Lifestyle/wellness creator (New Year's reset, winter getaway angle)
- Q2: Family travel creator (spring break, summer planning)
- Q3: Adventure/outdoor creator (peak season content)
- Q4: Food/wine creator (holiday entertaining, fall vibes)

**Ambassador Program:** Your top-performing creators get invited back annually with:
- Priority booking on new dates
- Commission on bookings they drive (track with unique referral links)
- Early access to new properties (when you expand)
- Feature on your 'As Seen On' page

**Creator Referral:** Ask creators to recommend other creators. The best target lists come from inside the creator community."

### MODULE 6 WRAP-UP [CANON 70D]
"**Spoke 6 — BUILT.** You have a vetted target list, outreach templates sent, a deal structure, and a plan for 4-6 creator stays per year.

**Homework:**
1. Book your first creator stay within 30 days
2. Set up your 'Creator Content' folder for repurposing
3. Plan your annual creator calendar (4-6 stays mapped to quarters)
4. After the first stay: measure reach, site visits, and bookings generated"
