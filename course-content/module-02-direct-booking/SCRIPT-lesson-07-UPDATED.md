## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:45) [CANON 70D]

"Most STR course traffic lessons tell you to run Google Ads. Dump $10-20 a day into paid clicks. Hope the algorithm is nice to you this week.

I'm going to tell you the opposite. Paid ads are a supplement, not your primary channel. Your primary traffic channel — the one that compounds, that costs nothing per click, that competitors can't buy you out of — is what we're calling the Local Guide Flywheel.

By the end of this lesson, you'll understand why /local-guide and /links matter more than any ad spend, what the flywheel looks like in action, and how to lay the foundation in Module 2 so that Module 2B can turn it into a traffic machine."

### WHY PAID ADS AREN'T THE ANSWER (0:45 - 2:30) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"Let me be clear — I'm not anti-ads. Paid ads have a place. But they are not what builds a durable STR business.

**[SLIDE: The Problem With Paid-First]**

- You stop paying, traffic stops
- Click costs for vacation rental keywords are $2-6 per click and rising
- Airbnb and Vrbo outbid you on every high-intent keyword
- Every click costs the same whether they book or not
- You don't own the audience — Google rents it to you
- The ad account is one policy change away from suspension

**[SLIDE: The Flywheel Alternative]**

- Content you publish stays up forever
- Each piece of content compounds — page 1 of Google for years
- Backlinks you earn from local partners build domain authority
- Your /local-guide becomes the authoritative resource for your region
- Google starts sending you traffic that didn't cost a penny
- Once the flywheel spins, it self-sustains

This is the pattern every durable vacation rental business follows. And it's the core of Module 2B."

### THE LOCAL GUIDE FLYWHEEL EXPLAINED (2:30 - 6:00) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"**[SLIDE: The Flywheel Diagram]**

Here's the flywheel:

1. **You publish local expertise** on /local-guide sub-pages — wineries, restaurants, things to do, day trips, seasonal guides, hidden gems
2. **Local partners link back** to you from their sites (/links exchange) because your guide mentions them
3. **Google sees authority signals** — quality content + backlinks + topical focus — and ranks you
4. **Travelers searching** 'Finger Lakes wineries' or 'best Seneca Lake restaurants' find your guide
5. **The guide mentions your property** naturally — 'our favorite base for exploring the trail' — with a link to /book
6. **Guests book** either directly or after signing up for /insider
7. **Guests leave reviews and referrals** that boost your brand searches, which boost SEO further
8. **Back to step 1** — you publish more content, with more authority, ranking faster

Every turn of the flywheel, the next turn gets easier. Unlike paid ads, where every turn costs the same.

**[SLIDE: Lakeside Landing FLX Real Example]**

Pull up lakesidelandingflx.com/local-guide. You'll see entries like:

- /local-guide/wineries — ranks for 'Seneca Lake wineries'
- /local-guide/restaurants — ranks for 'best restaurants Finger Lakes'
- /local-guide/things-to-do — ranks for 'things to do Seneca Lake'
- /local-guide/fall-foliage — ranks for 'Finger Lakes fall weekend'

Each of those pages pulls 50-500 organic visitors a month. Each page has an inline mention of Lakeside Landing as the recommended base — with a 'Book direct and save' CTA. Some of those visitors sign up for /insider. Some book immediately. The traffic is 100% free.

That's the pattern we lay the foundation for in this lesson. The full build happens in Module 2B."

### MODULE 2 FOUNDATION FOR THE FLYWHEEL (6:00 - 9:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Here's what you do NOW, in Module 2, to prep for Module 2B:

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Google Business Profile]**

**Foundation 1: Google Business Profile optimized for direct**

You set up GBP in Module 1. Now optimize it specifically for your direct booking site.

- Website URL → your direct booking /home, not your Airbnb listing
- Description: include 'book direct' and 'save 15% vs Airbnb' + 2-3 local keywords
- Photos: top 10 pro shots
- Posts: monthly updates with seasonal availability + /packages offers
- Reviews: ask every direct guest for a Google review in your post-stay sequence

**Foundation 2: /local-guide placeholder**

Your /local-guide URL is live as a placeholder. That's enough for now — Module 2B fills it in.

But add ONE thing today: an email capture on that placeholder page. 'Finger Lakes local guide coming soon. Get early access: [email].' That capture starts building your list even before the guide exists.

**Foundation 3: /links placeholder**

Same pattern. /links exists as a placeholder page. Module 2B turns it into the partner exchange engine.

**Foundation 4: Google Search Console setup**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: search.google.com/search-console]**

Add your site to Search Console. Verify ownership. Submit your sitemap. This is the dashboard that will tell you how /local-guide sub-pages are ranking when Module 2B goes live.

**Foundation 5: Schema markup on your property page**

Your property page should have structured data — vacation rental schema — so Google understands what it is. Lodgify adds this automatically. On a custom Next.js build, use JSON-LD in the page head. This is not optional if you care about ranking.

**Foundation 6: Internal linking strategy**

Your /home should link to /packages, /local-guide, and /insider above the fold. Your /[property-name] page should link to /packages, /add-ons, and /local-guide. Every page should give Google a path to every other important page. This is the internal scaffolding the flywheel runs on."

### PAID ADS AS A SUPPLEMENT (9:00 - 12:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Now — paid ads. Not zero. Just not primary.

**[SLIDE: The Minimum Viable Paid Setup]**

Three paid channels worth running, in priority order:

**1. Google Search for your property name ($2-5/day)**

When someone types 'Lakeside Landing FLX' into Google after seeing your Airbnb listing, you want YOUR direct site to be the first result. Airbnb often runs ads on your property name to re-capture that click. Defend it for a few dollars a day.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Brand search campaign setup in Google Ads]**

- Keyword: your property name (exact match)
- Ad copy: 'Book [Property Name] Direct — Save 15%'
- Landing page: /home or /[property-name]
- Budget: $2-5/day

**2. Google Search for niche long-tail queries ($5-10/day, optional)**

Not 'Finger Lakes vacation rental' — too expensive, Airbnb wins. Instead, try:
- '[your town] wine country rental'
- '[your town] lakefront retreat'
- 'book direct [your region]'

Set max CPC at $2. Pause anything that spends without converting.

**3. Retargeting via Meta pixel ($3-7/day, once you have traffic)**

Once /local-guide starts pulling organic visitors, retarget them with package offers. A visitor who read /local-guide/fall-foliage gets an Instagram ad for /packages/fall-wine-weekend. That's the bridge between free content and paid conversion.

**[SLIDE: Paid Budget Allocation]**

Total starting budget: $10-22/day ($300-660/month)
- Brand defense: 20%
- Niche long-tail search: 40%
- Retargeting: 40% (once traffic exists)

That's it. No Performance Max. No Display network. No broad-match keywords. Laser focus.

If you have budget beyond that, pour it into hiring a writer to produce more /local-guide content. That investment compounds. Paid clicks don't."

**[SLIDE: Referral Quick Wins]**

- 'Loved your stay? Share this with a friend. They save $50, you get $50 credit.'"
