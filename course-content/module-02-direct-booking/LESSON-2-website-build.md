### SITE ARCHITECTURE (2:30 - 4:00) [OBSBOT + SLIDES]

"Before we touch the builder, let's map the site.

**[SLIDE: 5-Page Site Architecture]**

Every direct booking site needs exactly 5 pages:

**1. Home Page**
The first thing visitors see. It must answer three questions in 5 seconds:
- What is this? (A vacation rental in [location])
- Why should I care? (Your hero statement — the experience, the view, the lifestyle)
- What do I do next? (Book Now button — clear, prominent, above the fold)

**2. Property Page**
The detailed listing — photos, description, amenities, reviews, availability calendar, pricing. This is your Airbnb listing but on YOUR site, with YOUR branding.

**3. Book Now / Availability Page**
The booking engine — date picker, guest count, pricing breakdown, payment form. This must be frictionless. Three clicks: pick dates, enter info, pay.

**4. About Page**
Who you are, why you started, what makes your property special. This builds trust. A guest who's never heard of you needs to feel safe booking outside of Airbnb. Your face, your story, and your reviews do that.

**5. FAQ / Contact Page**
Cancellation policy, check-in instructions, parking, pet policy, local area info. Answer every question a guest might have before they book. Fewer questions = faster bookings.

That's it. Five pages. Don't overcomplicate it. The goal is bookings, not a magazine."

### LIVE BUILD (4:00 - 14:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Let's build. Open Lodgify — or OwnerRez, or whichever platform you chose — and follow along.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Lodgify dashboard]**

**Step 1: Create your account and add your property (2 min)**

Go to lodgify.com, sign up for the Starter plan ($12/month). Enter your property details — name, address, type, bedrooms, bathrooms, max guests.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Account creation, property setup]**

**Step 2: Choose a template (1 min)**

Lodgify offers several vacation rental website templates. Pick one that's clean and modern — I recommend 'Starter' or 'Classic.' We'll customize it in a minute.

Don't spend 3 hours picking a template. Pick one and move on. Content matters more than template.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Template selection]**

**Step 3: Customize your Home Page (3 min)**

**Hero section:**
- Upload your best property photo as the hero image (the same hero shot from your Airbnb)
- Write your headline. Use this formula: [Experience] + [Location]
  Example: 'Your Private Beach Retreat in Destin' or 'Wake Up to Ocean Views — Steps from the Sand'
- Add a prominent 'Check Availability' or 'Book Now' button

**Below the hero:**
- Add 3-4 feature highlights: number of guests, bedrooms, key amenity, location
- Add a section with 2-3 of your best guest reviews
- Add a 'Book Direct & Save' callout — mention that booking on your site is cheaper than Airbnb

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Customizing hero, adding sections]**

**Step 4: Build your Property Page (3 min)**

- Upload ALL your professional photos (same ones from Airbnb, in the same optimized order)
- Copy your property description — but rewrite the opening for your brand voice (not Airbnb's template feel)
- Add your full amenity list
- Enable the availability calendar widget
- Enable the booking widget with pricing

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Property page setup, photo upload, calendar widget]**

**Step 5: Configure the Booking Engine (2 min)**

- Connect Stripe for payment processing (Lodgify walks you through this — it takes about 5 minutes to create a Stripe account if you don't have one)
- Set your nightly rate — remember, 5-10% BELOW your Airbnb rate (you're still earning more because no commission)
- Set minimum night stays
- Set cleaning fee
- Set damage deposit amount (if applicable)
- Enable instant booking or request-to-book (I recommend instant for higher conversion)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Stripe connection, rate setup, booking settings]**

**Step 6: About Page (1 min)**

- Add a photo of yourself (builds trust — guests are booking from a PERSON, not a faceless platform)
- Write 3-4 sentences about who you are and why you love hosting
- Mention how long you've been hosting and how many guests you've welcomed
- Add your contact email and phone number

**[SCREEN RECORDING: About page setup]**

**Step 7: FAQ Page (1 min)**

Add these 8 FAQs minimum:
1. How do I check in? (Instructions or smart lock code delivery)
2. What's the cancellation policy?
3. Is parking available?
4. Are pets allowed?
5. What's the check-in/out time?
6. Is there WiFi?
7. How far is the beach/downtown/attraction?
8. Who do I contact if I have an issue?

**[SCREEN RECORDING: FAQ page setup]**

**Step 8: Domain setup (1 min)**

Option A: Use Lodgify's free subdomain (yourname.lodgify.com) — works, but not premium.
Option B: Buy a custom domain ($10-15/year from Namecheap or Google Domains) and connect it.

I recommend a custom domain. Something like: StayAtSunsetBeach.com or SunsetBeachRetreat.com. Short, memorable, includes your property name.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Domain connection]**

**Step 9: Publish (30 seconds)**

Click publish. Your site is live. You now have a direct booking website."

### POST-BUILD OPTIMIZATION (14:00 - 16:00) [CANON 70D]

"Your site is live — but let's make it convert.

**[SLIDE: 7 Conversion Boosters]**

1. **'Book Direct & Save' badge** — Add a visible badge or banner that says 'Save 10-15% vs Airbnb — Book Direct.' This is your #1 conversion tool. Guests need a reason to book here instead of Airbnb.

2. **Reviews on every page** — Pull your best Airbnb reviews and display them on your direct site. Social proof is what makes strangers trust you.

3. **Mobile responsive** — Test your site on your phone RIGHT NOW. Over 60% of vacation rental traffic is mobile. If it's hard to book on a phone, you're losing more than half your potential guests.

4. **Fast loading** — Compress your images. Large photo files slow down your site. Use TinyPNG (free) to compress before uploading.

5. **Clear contact information** — Email and phone visible on every page. Guests booking outside Airbnb need reassurance they can reach you if something goes wrong.

6. **Secure payment badge** — Display 'Secure Payment by Stripe' near your booking form. Guests need to know their credit card is safe.

7. **Google Analytics** — Install GA4 (free) so you can track visitors, page views, and booking conversion rate. We cover measurement in Lesson 6."
