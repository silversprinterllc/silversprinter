## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:40) [CANON 70D]

"What if I told you that for every $100 a guest pays on Airbnb, you keep as little as $80? The rest goes to Airbnb — for doing nothing more than showing your listing in search results.

Now multiply that by every booking, every month, every year. On a property earning $60,000 annually through Airbnb, you're handing over $9,000 to $12,000 in commissions. Every. Single. Year.

That money could be yours. All of it. That's what a direct booking system does — it lets guests book directly with you, through your own website, with zero platform commission. This module builds that system from scratch."

### THE COMMISSION BLEED (0:40 - 3:00) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"Let's get specific about what platform dependency actually costs you.

**[SLIDE: 'The Commission Bleed']**

Airbnb's fee structure as of 2026: Airbnb charges hosts a flat 15.5% host-only service fee on every booking, with no separate guest service fee. You pay 15.5% of every booking.

But that's not the full picture. There are hidden costs:

**[SLIDE: Hidden Costs appearing one at a time]**

1. **The direct commission:** 15.5% per booking (2026 Airbnb host-only fee)
2. **The pricing pressure:** Airbnb's algorithm rewards competitive (lower) pricing. You're incentivized to undercharge.
3. **The data lock:** Airbnb hides guest contact information. You can't email them. You can't text them. You can't invite them back directly. Every repeat guest goes through Airbnb again — and you pay commission again.
4. **The algorithm risk:** One algorithm change can tank your visibility overnight. In 2024, Airbnb's shift to Guest Favorite caused thousands of hosts to lose ranking and bookings.
5. **The comparison trap:** On Airbnb, your listing sits next to 50 competitors. Guests comparison-shop on price. On your own site, you're the ONLY option.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Airbnb payout screen]**

Let me show you a real payout. Here's a booking for $1,200. After Airbnb's service fee, the host payout is $1,026. That's $174 gone — on a single booking.

Now multiply that. If you get 100 bookings per year at an average of $800 each, Airbnb takes approximately $10,400 to $12,800 depending on your fee model.

**[SLIDE: Annual Commission by Revenue Level]**

| Annual Revenue | Commission Lost (15.5% host-only, 2026) |
|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| $30,000 | $4,650 |
| $50,000 | $7,750 |
| $75,000 | $11,625 |
| $100,000 | $15,500 |

That's the commission bleed. And it happens every year, forever — unless you build a direct channel."

### THE DIRECT BOOKING ADVANTAGE (3:00 - 5:30) [CANON 70D]

"Now here's the other side. When a guest books directly through your website:

**[SLIDE: 5 Advantages appearing]**

**1. Zero commission.** You keep 100% minus payment processing (Stripe charges 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction). On an $800 booking, you pay about $23 to Stripe instead of $120 to Airbnb. You just saved $97.

**2. You own the guest relationship.** You have their name, email, phone number, trip dates, preferences. You can email them in 6 months to invite them back. You can send them a birthday offer. You can ask for a referral. They're YOUR customer.

**3. No competition on the page.** On your direct site, there's no 'Similar listings nearby' sidebar. No competitor carousel. No price comparison. It's just your property. The only decision is 'book or don't.'

**4. You control the brand experience.** Your website looks like YOUR brand — your colors, your photos, your voice. Not Airbnb's template. Premium positioning starts with brand control.

**5. Higher lifetime value per guest.** A guest who books direct and goes into your email system has a 30-40% chance of booking again within 18 months. An Airbnb guest who you never capture? Maybe 5%. And that repeat Airbnb booking costs you commission all over again.

**[SLIDE: The Goal]**

The goal is NOT to leave Airbnb. Airbnb is a powerful demand channel and it stays in your spoke portfolio. The goal is to shift 30-40% of your bookings to direct within the first 12 months. At that point, you're saving thousands in commissions while building a guest database that grows in value every single month."

### THE DIRECT BOOKING FUNNEL (5:30 - 8:00) [OBSBOT + SLIDES]

"Let me show you how a direct booking actually happens. It's a funnel — just like any sales process.

**[SLIDE: The Direct Booking Funnel — 5 stages]**

**Stage 1: Discovery**
A potential guest discovers your property. This happens through:
- Google search (they search 'vacation rental in [your city]')
- Social media (they see your TikTok or Instagram post)
- Referral (a past guest tells their friend)
- Google Vacation Rentals (they find you on Google Travel)
- An influencer post that links to your site

All of these spokes we built (or will build) funnel traffic to one place: your direct booking website.

**Stage 2: Landing**
The guest arrives on your website. They see your property, your photos, your availability calendar, your reviews, and your price. This is where first impressions matter — we'll build a conversion-optimized site in Lesson 2.

**Stage 3: Consideration**
The guest browses, checks dates, reads your description, looks at photos. They might compare with Airbnb. This is where your direct booking incentive matters — we offer a 'Book Direct & Save' rate that's 5-10% lower than Airbnb. The guest gets a better deal. You STILL earn more because you're not paying commission.

Let me show you the math:

**[SLIDE: Everybody Wins Math]**

Airbnb rate: $250/night
Guest pays on Airbnb: $250 (no separate guest service fee in 2026 — host absorbs the full 15.5% fee)
You receive from Airbnb: $250 - 15.5% host fee = $211.25

Direct booking rate: $237/night (5% less than Airbnb)
Guest pays direct: $237 (no service fee — they save nothing on rate, but no Airbnb hidden surcharges)
You receive: $237 - 2.9% Stripe fee = $230.13

You earn: $230 vs $211 = $19 MORE per night direct — AND ZERO acquisition cost for the next booking because you now own the relationship.

After the guest's second direct booking, you're ahead. After their third, you're WAY ahead. This is why direct booking is a long game that compounds.

**Stage 4: Booking**
The guest enters their dates, fills in their info, pays through Stripe. Confirmation email sent automatically. You receive a notification.

**Stage 5: Capture & Retain**
After the booking, the guest enters your email system. They get a welcome sequence, a pre-arrival guide, a mid-stay check-in, a post-stay thank you, and — months later — a 'come back' offer. This is Module 7 (Guest Network), but the capture starts here.

This is the funnel. Discovery → Landing → Consideration → Booking → Capture. Every lesson in this module builds one piece of this funnel."
