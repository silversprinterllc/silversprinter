### THE 4 METRICS THAT MATTER (0:20 - 2:00) [SLIDES]

"You do NOT need to become a data analyst. You need to track 4 numbers, once a month, for 10 minutes.

**[SLIDE: The 4 Direct Booking Metrics]**

**1. Website Traffic** — How many visitors came to your direct site this month? From where?
**2. Conversion Rate** — Of those visitors, what percentage actually booked? (Industry benchmark: 1-3%)
**3. Direct Booking Percentage** — What percentage of your total bookings came direct vs platforms?
**4. Commission Savings** — How much money did you save by NOT paying platform fees on direct bookings?

That's it. Four numbers. Let me show you how to track each one."

### GOOGLE ANALYTICS 4 SETUP (2:00 - 5:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"**[SCREEN RECORDING: Google Analytics]**

**Step 1:** Go to analytics.google.com and create a free account.

**Step 2:** Create a new property for your direct booking website.

**Step 3:** Copy the tracking code (called a 'Measurement ID' — starts with 'G-'). Paste it into your website platform:
- Lodgify: Settings > Integrations > Google Analytics > paste ID
- WordPress: Use a plugin like Site Kit by Google (free) or paste into header

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Pasting tracking ID into Lodgify]**

**Step 4:** Verify it's working. Visit your own site, then check GA4 'Real-time' report — you should see yourself as an active user.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Real-time report showing activity]**

**What to check monthly in GA4:**

Go to Reports > Acquisition > Traffic acquisition. This shows you:
- How many visitors you got
- Where they came from (Google search, social media, direct, referral, paid ads)
- Which traffic source converts best

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Walking through acquisition report]**

The traffic source with the highest conversion rate is where you should invest more. If Google Ads converts at 3% but Instagram sends traffic that converts at 0.5%, put more budget into Google Ads.

Also check: Reports > Engagement > Pages and screens. This shows which pages get the most views and how long people spend on them. If people view your property page but leave without booking, something on that page is broken — price, photos, description, or trust elements."

**[SLIDE: What to do with the data]**

**If traffic is low:** Write more blog posts, increase Google Ads budget, post more on social. The funnel needs more people at the top.

**If traffic is good but conversion is low:** Something on your site is broken. Check: Is the booking button prominent? Are your photos professional? Is the price competitive? Are reviews visible? Test one change per month.

**If direct booking % isn't growing:** Your traffic sources may be sending the wrong people. Check where traffic comes from and whether those visitors are actually your target guest.

**If everything is growing:** You're winning. Keep doing what you're doing. Consider increasing ad spend on the highest-converting channel."

### A/B TESTING BASICS (7:30 - 8:30) [CANON 70D]

"Once you have traffic, run simple A/B tests to improve conversion. Change ONE element at a time and measure the impact over 2-4 weeks.

**[SLIDE: What to A/B Test]**

Start with these — in order of impact:

1. **Hero image** — Test your top 2 property photos as the hero. The image guests see first has the biggest impact on whether they stay or leave.
2. **Headline** — Test two different headlines. One experience-focused, one amenity-focused.
3. **CTA button text** — Test 'Book Now' vs 'Check Availability' vs 'See Pricing.' Small words, big impact.
4. **Price display** — Test showing nightly rate vs showing 'From $X/night' vs showing weekly rate.

Don't test more than one thing at a time. Run each test for at least 2 weeks or 200 visitors — whichever comes first. Keep the winner, test the next element."
