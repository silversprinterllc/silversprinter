## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:30) [CANON 70D]

"Here's the most valuable asset in your STR business — and it's not the property. It's your guest list.

Every guest who stays at your property has a name, an email, a phone number, travel preferences, and a reason to come back. But if they booked on Airbnb, you have NONE of that. Airbnb keeps it.

This lesson is about capturing that data at every touchpoint — so you own the relationship, not the platform."

### WHY GUEST DATA MATTERS (0:30 - 2:00) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"**[SLIDE: 'The Most Expensive Guest Is the First One']**

It costs you money to acquire every new guest — through platform commissions, advertising, content creation, or time. The first booking is the most expensive.

But the second booking from the same guest? Almost free. No commission. No ad spend. Just an email that says 'ready to come back?'

The math is simple:
- Cost to acquire a new guest on Airbnb: $120-$200 (commission + fees)
- Cost to re-book a past guest via email: $0.01 (the cost of sending an email)

That's a 10,000x difference in acquisition cost. But you can only email them if you HAVE their email. And you can only have their email if you capture it.

**[SLIDE: 5 Capture Points]**

There are 5 moments where you can capture guest data:
1. **WiFi login** — Guest connects to your WiFi, you collect their email (StayFi)
2. **Pre-arrival form** — Guest fills out info before check-in
3. **Booking form** — Guest provides info during direct booking (automatic)
4. **Post-stay survey** — Guest provides feedback and additional data
5. **Website opt-in** — Visitor downloads a free guide or enters email for a discount

We're going to set up all five."

### STAYFI SETUP (2:00 - 5:30) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"StayFi is the tool that captures guest emails through your property WiFi. When a guest connects to WiFi, they see a branded login page where they enter their email address. That email goes straight to your database.

It's passive. It's automatic. And it captures guests who booked through ANY platform — including Airbnb.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: stayfi.com]**

**Step 1: Sign up for StayFi ($15/month)**

Go to stayfi.com and create an account. The base plan covers one property with WiFi marketing and up to 200 contacts.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Account creation]**

**Step 2: Connect your router**

StayFi works with most standard WiFi routers. You connect your existing router to StayFi's system, which adds the branded login page. StayFi provides step-by-step instructions for your specific router model.

If your router isn't compatible, StayFi sells pre-configured routers for about $99 that plug right in.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Router setup instructions]**

**Step 3: Customize your splash page**

This is the page guests see when they connect to WiFi. Customize it with:
- Your property name and logo (or photo)
- A welcome message: 'Welcome to [Property Name]! Connect to WiFi below.'
- Email capture field (required to connect)
- Optional: phone number field, name field
- Optional: 'Subscribe to get return guest discounts' checkbox

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Customizing splash page]**

Keep it simple. Don't ask for too much — email is the priority. Every additional field reduces completion rate.

**Step 4: Set up email integration**

StayFi integrates with major email platforms — Mailchimp, ConvertKit, ActiveCampaign, and others. Connect your email platform so captured emails automatically flow into your list.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Email integration setup]**

If you don't have an email platform yet, StayFi has built-in email marketing — basic but functional. You can start with StayFi's native emails and upgrade to a dedicated platform later.

**Step 5: Test**

Connect to your own WiFi. You should see the branded splash page. Enter a test email. Verify it appears in your StayFi dashboard and your email platform.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Testing the flow]**

From this point forward, every guest who connects to WiFi at your property automatically enters your database. You'll capture 70-90% of guests this way — most people connect to WiFi within the first hour of arrival."

### PRE-ARRIVAL FORM (5:30 - 7:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Next, let's capture data BEFORE the guest arrives.

Set up a pre-arrival form that your PMS sends automatically 3-5 days before check-in. Most PMS platforms (Hostaway, Hospitable, OwnerRez) support automated pre-arrival messages.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: PMS message template setup]**

The message:
'Hi [Guest Name]! We're excited to host you at [Property Name] starting [Date]. To make your stay perfect, please fill out this quick form: [LINK]'

The form captures:
- Full name (may not match the booking name if a family member booked)
- Email address (critical — gets you their direct email even for Airbnb bookings)
- Phone number
- Trip purpose (vacation, family reunion, anniversary, work trip)
- Special occasions during the stay (birthday, anniversary — lets you send surprise touches)
- Special requests (extra towels, crib, high chair, early check-in)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Building the form in Google Forms or Typeform]**

Use Google Forms (free) or Typeform (free tier). Keep it to 6-8 fields max. Make it feel helpful, not invasive — 'help us customize your stay' not 'give us your data.'

The trip purpose and special occasion fields are gold. When you know someone is celebrating an anniversary, you can leave a $10 bottle of wine with a card. That guest will rave about you in their review — and book direct next time."

### POST-STAY SURVEY (7:00 - 8:30) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"After checkout, send an automated post-stay survey. This serves three purposes:

1. Captures any guest data you missed
2. Generates feedback to improve your property
3. Sets up the review request (we build this in Module 7)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Survey setup]**

Send this 24 hours after checkout. The message:
'Hi [Guest Name], thank you for staying at [Property Name]! We'd love your feedback — it takes 2 minutes: [LINK]'

The survey:
- Overall experience rating (1-5 stars)
- What did you love most?
- What could we improve?
- Would you stay with us again? (Yes/No)
- Would you recommend us to friends? (Yes/No)
- Email address (if not already captured)
- 'Join our guest list for exclusive return discounts' checkbox

This survey does double duty — it's feedback AND it's a warm handoff to your review request (which we send 24 hours after the survey, in Module 7)."

### BUILDING YOUR DATABASE (8:30 - 10:00) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"Now let's organize all this data.

**[SLIDE: Guest Database Structure]**

Your guest database should track:

| Field | Source | Why It Matters |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Name | Booking / pre-arrival form | Personalization |
| Email | StayFi / booking / form | Primary communication channel |
| Phone | Pre-arrival form | SMS marketing (Module 2 Lesson 4) |
| Stay dates | PMS | Timing for return offers |
| Property | PMS | Relevant for multi-property operators |
| Booking source | PMS | Track which channel they came from |
| Trip purpose | Pre-arrival form | Segment for targeted marketing |
| Review status | Post-stay survey | Track who reviewed, who didn't |
| Lifetime value | Calculated | Total revenue from this guest across all stays |

**[SLIDE: Segmentation]**

Segment your guests into groups:
- **First-timers** — need nurturing, review request, referral ask
- **Repeat guests** — VIP treatment, loyalty offers, early access to dates
- **Referrers** — guests who've referred others, deserve recognition
- **High-value** — guests who booked premium dates, longer stays, or added experiences
- **By trip type** — families, couples, business, groups (for targeted offers)

Your email platform (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, etc.) handles segmentation with tags and lists. We'll set up the actual email sequences in the next lesson."

- Booking form (direct bookings capture everything)
- Post-stay survey (catches anyone you missed + generates feedback)
- Website opt-in (captures prospective guests who haven't booked yet)

See you there."
