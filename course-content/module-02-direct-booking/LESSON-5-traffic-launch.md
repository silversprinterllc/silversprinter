## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:30) [CANON 70D]

"Your website is live. Your funnels are automated. Your capture system is running. There's just one problem — nobody knows it exists.

A direct booking site without traffic is a store with no front door. This lesson opens the door. We're covering 6 traffic channels — ranked by ROI — that will drive guests to YOUR site instead of Airbnb's."

### THE 6 TRAFFIC CHANNELS (0:30 - 2:00) [SLIDES]

"**[SLIDE: Traffic channels ranked by ROI]**

Here they are, ranked from highest ROI to lowest:

1. **Google Business Profile** — Free. Highest intent. Drives 10-20 direct bookings/year.
2. **SEO Blog Posts** — Free. Compounds forever. Takes 2-3 months to kick in.
3. **Google Vacation Rentals** — Free (set up in Module 1). Appears in Google Travel search.
4. **Social Media** — Free (time investment). Builds brand. Drives discovery.
5. **Referral/Word of Mouth** — Free. Highest conversion rate of any channel.
6. **Google Ads** — Paid ($10-20/day). Immediate traffic. Best for testing.

Notice: 5 out of 6 are free. You don't need a big ad budget. You need smart content in the right places."

### CHANNEL 1: GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE (2:00 - 4:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"You set up your Google Business Profile in Module 1. Now let's optimize it specifically for driving direct bookings.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Google Business Profile dashboard]**

**Optimization 1: Website link**
Make sure your website URL points to your DIRECT BOOKING SITE — not your Airbnb listing. This is the most common mistake. Every click from Google should land on YOUR site.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Updating website URL]**

**Optimization 2: Description with keywords**
Rewrite your business description to include the keywords travelers search for:
'[Property Name] is a [X]-bedroom vacation rental in [City], [State], [distance] from [top attraction]. Features include [top 3 amenities]. Book direct at [website URL] for the best rates — save up to 15% vs booking platforms.'

The phrase 'book direct' and 'save' in your Google description is powerful — travelers who find you on Google are already looking to book outside platforms.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Updating description]**

**Optimization 3: Photos**
Upload your top 10 professional property photos. Google prioritizes listings with more photos in local search.

**Optimization 4: Reviews**
Google reviews matter. Ask every direct booking guest to leave a Google review in addition to (or instead of) an Airbnb review. Your post-stay email sequence should include a Google review link.

**Optimization 5: Posts**
Google Business allows you to post updates — like social media for your Google listing. Post monthly: seasonal availability, special offers, property updates, local events. Each post keeps your listing active and signals relevance to Google."

### CHANNEL 2: SEO BLOG POSTS (4:00 - 7:00) [CANON 70D + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Blog posts are the long game. A well-written post targeting 'best vacation rental in [your city]' can drive traffic for YEARS. Here's how to write posts that actually rank.

**[SLIDE: 4 Blog Post Templates]**

I've included 4 ready-to-customize blog post templates in your course resources. Here are the topics:

**Post 1: '[City] Vacation Rental Guide — Everything You Need to Know'**
Target keyword: '[city] vacation rental'
Length: 1,500-2,000 words
Content: Area overview, best times to visit, what to do, why your property is the perfect base. Include your booking link throughout.

**Post 2: 'Top [10] Things to Do in [City] for Families/Couples/Groups'**
Target keyword: 'things to do in [city]'
Length: 1,500-2,000 words
Content: Local attractions, restaurants, activities. Naturally mention your property as the ideal home base. This post targets travelers in the PLANNING stage — before they've booked anywhere.

**Post 3: '[City] vs [Nearby City] — Which Is Better for Your Vacation?'**
Target keyword: '[city] vacation' and '[nearby city] vacation'
Length: 1,200-1,500 words
Content: Comparison guide that positions your market as the better choice. Captures travelers who are deciding between destinations.

**Post 4: 'Why Book Direct — Save 15% on Your [City] Vacation Rental'**
Target keyword: '[city] vacation rental direct booking'
Length: 800-1,000 words
Content: Explains the benefits of booking direct vs platforms. Perfect for guests who find your Airbnb listing and then Google your property name.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Blog post setup in Lodgify or WordPress]**

If you're using Lodgify, it has a built-in blog feature. If you're on WordPress (via OwnerRez or Boostly), you have full blog capabilities.

Publish all 4 posts within your first month. Then add 1-2 posts per month going forward. Each post is a fishing line in the water — the more lines, the more fish."

### CHANNEL 3-4: SOCIAL & REFERRALS (7:00 - 9:00) [CANON 70D]

"We cover social media in depth in Module 5, and referral programs in Module 7. But here are the quick wins for driving traffic NOW:

**[SLIDE: Social Quick Wins]**

**Social:**
- Add your direct booking URL to your Instagram bio, TikTok bio, and Pinterest profile
- Every social post should end with 'Book direct at [URL] and save'
- Pin a 'Book Direct & Save' post to the top of your Instagram and TikTok profiles
- Share your 4 blog posts across all social platforms

**Referrals:**
- Your referral email sequence is already running (Lesson 4)
- Add a 'Refer a Friend' page to your website with the referral offer
- Give every departing guest a physical card: 'Loved your stay? Share this with a friend. They save $50, you get $50 credit.' Include your direct booking URL and a QR code."

### CHANNEL 5: GOOGLE ADS (9:00 - 11:30) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Google Ads is the only paid channel I recommend starting with. It's the fastest way to get traffic while your SEO builds.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Google Ads setup]**

Here's the $10-20/day starter campaign:

**Step 1: Create a Google Ads account** at ads.google.com.

**Step 2: Create a Search campaign** (not Display, not Performance Max — just Search).

**Step 3: Target keywords:**
- '[your city] vacation rental'
- '[your city] beach house rental' (or cabin, condo, etc.)
- '[your city] Airbnb alternative'
- 'book direct [your city] rental'
- '[your property name]' (people who Google your property name after seeing it on Airbnb)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Adding keywords]**

**Step 4: Write your ad.**

Headline 1: '[City] Vacation Rental — Book Direct & Save 15%'
Headline 2: '[X] Bedrooms | Private Pool | Steps to Beach'
Headline 3: 'No Booking Fees — Best Price Guaranteed'
Description: 'Skip the platform fees. Book [Property Name] directly and save up to 15%. [X] bedrooms, sleeps [X]. [Top amenity]. Check availability now.'

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Writing ad copy]**

**Step 5: Set budget and location targeting.**

- Daily budget: $10-20 (start small, increase what works)
- Location: Target a 50-mile radius around your property PLUS major feeder cities (where your guests fly in from)
- Schedule: Run ads 24/7 initially, then optimize to peak search times

**Step 6: Set your landing page** to your direct booking site home page or property page.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Final campaign settings]**

**Step 7: Launch and wait 7 days.** Don't touch it for a week. Let Google's algorithm learn who clicks. After 7 days, check which keywords are performing and cut the ones that aren't.

At $15/day, you're spending $450/month. If that generates even 2-3 direct bookings per month at $250/night average, you're earning $500-$750 while saving another $75-$112 in Airbnb commissions. The math works immediately."
