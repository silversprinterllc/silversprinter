### THE COMMISSION BLEED — AND WHAT YOU'RE ACTUALLY BUYING BACK (0:50 - 3:00) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"Let's get specific about what platform dependency actually costs you — and what you're actually buying when you build direct.

**[SLIDE: 'The Commission Bleed']**

Airbnb's fee structure (as of 2026): a flat 15.5% host-only service fee on every booking, with no separate guest service fee. On a property earning $60K annually, that's $9,300 a year, forever, unless you build a direct channel.

**[SLIDE: Hidden Costs]**

But the commission is only the headline number. Here's what's actually leaking:

1. **Direct commission** — 15.5% per booking (2026 Airbnb host-only fee)
2. **Pricing pressure** — the algorithm rewards you for undercutting yourself
3. **Data lock** — you don't own your guest's contact info, so every repeat booking pays commission again
4. **Algorithm risk** — one change to Guest Favorite or search ranking can crater your visibility overnight
5. **The comparison trap** — on Airbnb, you're one of 50 thumbnails. On your own site, you're the only option
6. **The ecosystem tax** — this is the one nobody talks about. Every guest Airbnb 'owns' is a guest you can't email, can't segment, can't offer a package to, can't invite into your loyalty program, can't ask for a referral. You're not just losing 15% — you're losing the ability to ever compound that relationship.

**[SLIDE: Annual Commission by Revenue Level]**

| Annual Revenue | Commission Lost (15%) | Direct Equivalent You're Missing |
|---|---|---|
| $30,000 | $4,500 | Plus zero email list, zero repeat flywheel |
| $50,000 | $7,500 | Plus no package revenue |
| $75,000 | $11,250 | Plus no loyalty program |
| $100,000 | $15,000 | Plus no referral compounding |

That's what you're fighting. And that's what your direct booking site is designed to reclaim."

### THE DIRECT BOOKING ADVANTAGE (5:30 - 7:30) [CANON 70D]

"Now the specific advantages of shifting bookings to direct:

**[SLIDE: 5 Advantages]**

**1. Zero commission.** Stripe is 2.9% + $0.30. On an $800 booking that's $23 instead of $120 to Airbnb. $97 saved per booking.

**2. You own the guest relationship.** Name, email, phone, trip dates, preferences, special occasions. You can email them in 6 months. You can segment them by stay type. You can invite them into your Insider program.

**3. No competition on the page.** On your site, you're not sitting next to 50 similar listings. There is no 'Similar listings nearby' carousel. There is only your property.

**4. You control the brand experience.** Your typography, your photography, your voice. When a guest lands on lakesidelandingflx.com, they feel like they've arrived at a specific place with a specific identity — not a standardized Airbnb template.

**5. Higher lifetime value per guest.** Direct guest with email capture: 30-40% re-book within 18 months. Airbnb guest with no capture: closer to 5% — and even that repeat booking pays commission.

**[SLIDE: The Goal]**

The goal is NOT to leave Airbnb. Airbnb stays in your portfolio as a demand spoke. The goal is to shift 30-40% of your bookings to direct within the first 12 months — AND to build the ecosystem that makes those direct guests worth 3-5x what an Airbnb guest is worth over their lifetime."

### THE DIRECT BOOKING FUNNEL (7:30 - 9:30) [OBSBOT + SLIDES]

"Here's the funnel every lesson in this module builds a piece of.

**[SLIDE: 5-Stage Funnel]**

**Stage 1: Discovery**
A guest finds you. Through Google (organic or ads), social media, a referral, Google Vacation Rentals, or — the big one we're building in Module 2B — your local guide content ranking in search.

**Stage 2: Landing**
They arrive on your site. Home page or property page. This is where Lesson 2 lives.

**Stage 3: Consideration**
They browse, check dates, compare. Your Book Direct & Save offer + Insider pricing + package options kill the Airbnb comparison. Let me show you the math:

**[SLIDE: Everybody Wins Math]**

Airbnb rate: $250/night
Guest pays on Airbnb: $250 (no separate guest service fee in 2026 — host absorbs the full 15.5%)
You receive: $250 - 15.5% = $211.25

Direct rate: $237/night (5% below Airbnb)
Guest pays direct: $237 (no service fee)
You receive: $237 - 2.9% Stripe = $230.13

You earn $19 MORE per night direct. Plus you gain the email, the segment, the ability to package and upsell, and the lifetime value multiplier. The direct channel wins on economics from booking one — and compounds from there.

**Stage 4: Booking**
Dates + info + Stripe payment. Confirmation email auto-fires.

**Stage 5: Capture & Retain**
Welcome sequence, pre-arrival guide, mid-stay check-in, post-stay thank you, review request, referral ask, and — 6 months later — the 'come back' offer. The guest enters the database. Every subsequent touch is commission-free."
