### THE 5 EMAIL SEQUENCES (0:30 - 3:30) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"Here are the 5 sequences every STR operator needs. We provide full copy-paste templates for each one in your course resources.

**[SLIDE: The 5 Sequences]**

**Sequence 1: Welcome Series (triggered when a guest books)**
- Email 1 (immediately): Booking confirmation + what to expect
- Email 2 (3 days before arrival): Pre-arrival guide — check-in instructions, local tips, WiFi info, house rules
- Email 3 (day of arrival): Welcome message — 'Your home is ready! Here's everything you need.'

This sequence sets expectations, reduces anxiety (guests worry about non-platform bookings), and establishes you as a professional.

**Sequence 2: Mid-Stay Check-In (triggered 2 days into stay)**
- Email 1: 'How's everything going? Need anything?'
- Purpose: Catch problems before they become bad reviews. Show you care. Offer restaurant recommendations or experience suggestions (these can be affiliate links — passive income).

One email. Two sentences. Massive impact on reviews.

**Sequence 3: Post-Stay + Review Request (triggered 24 hours after checkout)**
- Email 1 (24 hours post-checkout): Thank you + review request link
- Email 2 (72 hours post-checkout, if no review): Gentle reminder + direct link to leave review
- Email 3 (7 days post-checkout): 'We'd love to hear from you' + incentive (10% off next stay if they leave a review)

This sequence generates a 40%+ review rate. We provide the exact subject lines and copy that get opens and clicks.

**Sequence 4: Return Guest Offer (triggered 6 months after checkout)**
- Email 1 (6 months post-stay): 'It's been a while — ready to come back?' + 10-15% off direct booking
- Email 2 (7 months, if no booking): 'Your favorite dates are still open' + seasonal nudge
- Email 3 (10 months, if no booking): 'Anniversary of your stay' + final offer

This is your money sequence. Reactivating a past guest costs nearly nothing and generates commission-free revenue.

**Sequence 5: Referral Ask (triggered 30 days after checkout)**
- Email 1: 'Know someone who'd love [Property Name]? Share this link — they get $50 off, you get $50 credit.'
- Includes a unique referral link for tracking

This turns happy guests into your unpaid sales team."

### BUILDING THE SEQUENCES (3:30 - 9:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Let me show you how to set these up. I'm using ConvertKit — but the process is almost identical in Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, or whatever email platform you chose.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: ConvertKit/Mailchimp dashboard]**

**Setting up Sequence 1: Welcome Series**

Step 1: Create a new automation. Trigger: 'When subscriber is added to [New Booking] tag.'

Your PMS or booking system adds this tag when a booking is confirmed. If your PMS integrates directly with your email platform, this is automatic. If not, you can use Zapier ($20/mo) to connect them.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Creating automation trigger]**

Step 2: Add Email 1 — Booking Confirmation.

I'm going to paste in the template we provide. Let me walk through it.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Pasting email template, highlighting key sections]**

Subject line: 'You're booked! Here's what happens next at [Property Name]'

Body:
- Personal greeting using guest's first name
- Confirmation of dates and property
- What to expect before arrival (they'll receive a pre-arrival guide)
- Your contact info (email + phone)
- A warm, personal sign-off

Key elements: The subject line creates certainty (they may be nervous booking outside Airbnb). The body establishes professionalism and accessibility. Your phone number builds trust.

Step 3: Add a delay — wait until 3 days before check-in. Then send Email 2: Pre-Arrival Guide.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Adding delay and second email]**

This email includes:
- Check-in time and instructions (smart lock code, key location, etc.)
- WiFi network and password
- Parking instructions
- 3-5 local recommendations (restaurants, activities, attractions)
- House rules summary
- Emergency contact info
- Link to your digital guidebook (if you have one — Module 9)

Step 4: Add another delay — day of arrival. Send Email 3: Welcome.

'Your home is ready! [Short personal message]. Everything you need is in the pre-arrival guide. Text me at [number] if you need anything at all. Enjoy your stay!'

Short. Warm. Accessible.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Final email in sequence, activating automation]**

**Setting up Sequence 3: Post-Stay + Review Request**

This is the highest-ROI sequence. Let me set it up.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: New automation]**

Trigger: 'When subscriber is added to [Checked Out] tag.' Your PMS adds this tag at checkout.

Email 1 — 24 hours after checkout:

Subject: '[Guest Name], thank you for staying at [Property Name]!'

Body:
- Thank them specifically (reference their trip if possible)
- Ask for a review: 'If you enjoyed your stay, a quick review means the world to us and helps other travelers find [Property Name].'
- Direct link to leave a review (Airbnb review link, Google review link, or both)
- Short and sincere — no more than 5-6 sentences

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Pasting review request template]**

Email 2 — 72 hours after checkout (only if no review yet):

Subject: 'Quick favor? It takes 2 minutes'

Body: Shorter version of the same ask. Include the direct review link again. Mention that reviews help small businesses like yours compete with big hotel chains (this appeals to guests' desire to support local/small).

Email 3 — 7 days after checkout:

Subject: 'Your thoughts on [Property Name]?'

Body: Final ask. Include incentive: '10% off your next direct booking as a thank you for sharing your feedback.'

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Complete automation activated]**

Now let's do the Return Guest and Referral sequences quickly — same process, different timing.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Fast setup of remaining sequences — montage style]**

Return Guest: Trigger at 6 months post-checkout. Three emails spaced 1 month apart.
Referral: Trigger at 30 days post-checkout. Single email with referral link."

### SMS FLOWS (9:00 - 11:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Email is your primary channel. SMS is your backup and booster.

**[SLIDE: 3 SMS Flows]**

Most guests open texts within 3 minutes. Email open rates are 20-30%. SMS open rates are 95%+. Use SMS sparingly and for high-value messages only.

**SMS Flow 1: Booking Confirmation**
Triggered immediately after booking.
'Hi [Name]! Your stay at [Property] is confirmed for [Dates]. Check-in details coming via email. Text this number anytime if you need anything! - [Your Name]'

**SMS Flow 2: Check-In Reminder**
Triggered morning of check-in.
'Welcome day! Check-in is at [Time]. Your access code is [Code]. WiFi: [Network] / [Password]. Have a great stay!'

**SMS Flow 3: Review Request**
Triggered 24 hours after checkout.
'Hi [Name], thanks for staying with us! If you have 2 minutes, a review would mean a lot: [Link]. Thanks! - [Your Name]'

Set these up in your PMS — most PMS platforms (Hostaway, Hospitable, OwnerRez) have built-in SMS functionality, or you can use Twilio for custom SMS.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: PMS SMS template setup]**

Keep SMS messages under 160 characters when possible. No marketing fluff. Just useful information delivered at the right moment."
