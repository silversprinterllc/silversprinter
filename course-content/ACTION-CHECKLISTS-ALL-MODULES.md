# MODULE 3: DYNAMIC PRICING & REVENUE STRATEGY | ACTION CHECKLIST

Complete ALL items below before moving to Module 4.
Watching the module won't improve your metrics. Taking action will.

## ACTIONS

[ ] 1. Sign up for PriceLabs and connect to your PMS — Create a PriceLabs account ($19.99/month per listing). Connect it to your PMS or channel manager (OwnerRez, Hospitable, Guesty, etc.). Verify your listing appears in PriceLabs with historical data imported. Cost: ~$20-40/month depending on listings.

[ ] 2. Set base prices for all seasons — In PriceLabs, configure your base price for each season using the Base Price Formula from the module. Example: Lakeside Landing base prices might be $800 (off-season), $1,200 (shoulder), $1,800 (peak summer). Smooth Sailing: $325 (off-season), $425 (shoulder), $525 (peak). Set these in the Customization tab.

[ ] 3. Configure minimum and maximum price guardrails — Set your absolute floor price (the lowest you will ever accept) and ceiling price for each listing. Example: Lakeside Landing min $650, max $2,200. Smooth Sailing min $275, max $625. These prevent algorithmic errors from pricing you too low or unrealistically high.

[ ] 4. Set event-based pricing rules for 5+ local events — Identify at least 5 annual events in your market that drive demand (e.g., Finger Lakes Wine Festival, NASCAR at Watkins Glen, college graduation weekends, Fourth of July, fall foliage peak). Create manual price overrides or date-specific rules in PriceLabs for each event with a minimum 25% premium over base price.

[ ] 5. Configure length-of-stay discounts — Set weekly discount (7+ nights: 10-15% off) and monthly discount (28+ nights: 20-30% off) in PriceLabs and on each platform. For luxury properties like Lakeside Landing, keep weekly discounts modest (10%); for mid-range properties like Smooth Sailing, you can go up to 15%.

[ ] 6. Set orphan-day gap filling rules — In PriceLabs, enable gap-night pricing. Configure: 1-night gaps get a 15-20% discount, 2-night gaps get a 10-15% discount. Set a minimum stay of 1 night for orphan days only. This fills revenue-killing gaps between bookings.

[ ] 7. Run a 90-day pricing audit — Review PriceLabs' recommended prices for the next 90 days. Override any dates that look wrong. Confirm pricing looks rational for weekends vs. weekdays, events, and seasonal transitions. Export the 90-day pricing calendar as a PDF for your records.

[ ] 8. Record your "Before" ADR — Pull your average daily rate from the last 90 days (from your PMS or Airbnb earnings). Write this number down. You will compare it against your "After" ADR in 90 days.
