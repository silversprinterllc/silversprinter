# LESSON 4: Marketing & Loyalty Automation

## Production Notes
- **Format:** OBSBOT screen recording (email platform automation builder) + Canon 70D (strategy)
- **Target Length:** 8-10 minutes

## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:20) [CANON 70D]
"You built email sequences in Module 2. Now we wire them into your PMS so they fire based on real booking events — and add the loyalty and referral automations that turn one-time guests into lifetime customers."

### REVIEW AUTOMATION (0:20 - 2:30) [SCREEN RECORDING]

Configure automated review collection:
- PMS triggers [Checked Out] tag → email platform sends review request sequence (3 emails over 7 days)
- Platform-specific: Airbnb review link vs Google review link vs both
- SMS backup: if no review after 72 hours, send SMS review request
- Track review rate in your monthly dashboard (target: 40%+)
- Handle negative reviews: template responses for 3-star and 4-star reviews (empathetic, solution-focused, invite them to contact you)

### RETURN GUEST AUTOMATION (2:30 - 4:30) [SCREEN RECORDING]

Configure the "come back" machine:
- 6 months after checkout → "Time to come back" email with 10-15% direct booking discount
- Personalize by season: "Summer's coming — your favorite dates are open"
- Personalize by trip type (from pre-arrival form data): families get family-focused messaging, couples get romantic getaway messaging
- Track: how many return bookings per quarter, what's the average discount cost vs acquisition cost savings

### REFERRAL PROGRAM SETUP (4:30 - 6:00) [SCREEN RECORDING]

Configure automated referral tracking:
- Create unique referral links per guest (most email platforms support this with merge tags)
- "Give $50, Get $50" structure: referrer gets $50 credit, new guest gets $50 off first direct booking
- Automated notification when someone uses a referral link
- Automated credit applied to referrer's next booking
- Monthly email to past guests reminding them about the referral program

### SEASONAL CAMPAIGN CALENDAR (6:00 - 7:30) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

**[SLIDE: 6 Campaigns Per Year]**

Pre-schedule 6 email campaigns to your guest list:

| Month | Campaign | Subject Line |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| January | "Early Bird Summer" | Lock in summer dates before they're gone |
| March | "Spring Break Special" | Last-minute availability + 10% off |
| May | "Summer is HERE" | Peak season dates going fast |
| August | "Fall Getaway" | Shoulder season deals, perfect weather |
| October | "Holiday Season" | Thanksgiving/Christmas/NYE availability |
| December | "New Year, New Trip" | Book your 2026 getaway now |

Write all 6 now using our templates. Schedule them in your email platform. They send automatically on the dates you set — zero work in the moment.

### CLOSE (7:30 - 8:30) [CANON 70D]

**Homework:**
1. Verify review request automation is connected (PMS → email platform → 3-email sequence)
2. Activate return guest sequence (6-month trigger)
3. Set up referral link system in your email platform
4. Write and schedule all 6 seasonal campaigns using templates
5. Add review tracking to your monthly dashboard
