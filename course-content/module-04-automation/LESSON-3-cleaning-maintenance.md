# LESSON 3: Cleaning & Maintenance Automation

## Production Notes
- **Format:** OBSBOT screen recording (Turno setup) + Canon 70D (strategy/close)
- **Target Length:** 8-10 minutes
- **Screen Recording:** Full Turno setup, cleaning checklist creation, maintenance workflow

---

## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:20) [CANON 70D]
"No more group texts to your cleaner. No more 'are you available Friday?' No more wondering if the house was actually cleaned. We're installing Turno — and every checkout will automatically schedule a cleaning, notify your cleaner, and verify completion with photos."

### TURNO SETUP (0:20 - 4:30) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

**Step 1:** Create Turno account (free for basic, $8/mo/property for Pro)
**Step 2:** Connect to your PMS — Turno pulls your booking calendar automatically
**Step 3:** Add your cleaning team — name, phone, email, availability, pay rate
**Step 4:** Set auto-scheduling rules:
- Guest checks out → cleaning auto-assigned to next available cleaner
- Notification sent to cleaner with property address, access info, and checklist
- If cleaner declines within 1 hour → auto-reassign to backup cleaner
**Step 5:** Create your cleaning checklist (template provided — 30+ items by room)

### PHOTO VERIFICATION (4:30 - 5:30) [SCREEN RECORDING]

Enable photo verification in Turno Pro:
- Cleaner takes photos of each completed room
- Photos uploaded through Turno app
- You get a notification with photo gallery when cleaning is marked complete
- You verify from your phone in 2 minutes — no need to visit the property

### INVENTORY MANAGEMENT (5:30 - 6:30) [SCREEN RECORDING]

Set up supply tracking:
- Create inventory list: toilet paper, paper towels, trash bags, soap, shampoo, coffee, etc.
- Set minimum quantities (e.g., alert when under 5 rolls of toilet paper)
- Cleaner reports low inventory during turnover → you get alerted → restock

### MAINTENANCE WORKFLOW (6:30 - 8:00) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

For maintenance issues (broken appliance, plumbing, HVAC):

**[SLIDE: Maintenance Workflow]**

1. Guest reports issue (through mid-stay check-in message or direct contact)
2. You assess severity via text/photo from guest
3. Non-urgent: Schedule vendor for next available slot
4. Urgent: Contact emergency vendor list (create this NOW — plumber, electrician, HVAC, locksmith, handyman)
5. Vendor dispatched → issue resolved → guest notified → follow up

**Template provided:** Emergency Vendor Contact Sheet — fill in with your local contacts before you need them.

### CLOSE (8:00 - 8:45) [CANON 70D]

**Homework:**
1. Set up Turno and connect to PMS
2. Add your cleaning team with availability and pay rates
3. Create your cleaning checklist using our 30+ item template
4. Enable photo verification
5. Build your Emergency Vendor Contact Sheet (plumber, electrician, HVAC, locksmith, handyman)
6. Set up supply inventory tracking
