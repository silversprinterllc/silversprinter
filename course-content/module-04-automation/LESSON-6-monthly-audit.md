# LESSON 6: Monthly Automation Audit

## Production Notes
- **Format:** OBSBOT screen recording (running the full audit live) + Canon 70D (module wrap-up)
- **Target Length:** 8-10 minutes
- **Screen Recording:** Walk through the complete monthly audit checklist with real dashboards

---

## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:15) [CANON 70D]
"Systems drift. What worked last month might not work next month. This lesson installs a 30-minute monthly ritual that keeps every automation running tight — and identifies one improvement to make each month."

### THE 30-MINUTE MONTHLY AUDIT (0:15 - 6:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"I'm going to run the full audit live, right now, on screen. Follow along with the Monthly Automation Audit Checklist.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Opening the checklist]**

**Block 1: Guest Messaging (5 minutes)**
- Check PMS message delivery report — are all 8 auto-messages sending?
- Review response time metric — still under 1 hour average?
- Scan AI smart reply log — any bad responses that need correction?
- Read last 5 guest reviews — any mention of communication issues?
- Action: Fix any broken triggers, update FAQ if new questions appeared

**[SCREEN RECORDING: PMS message delivery stats]**

**Block 2: Cleaning & Turnovers (5 minutes)**
- Check Turno completion rate — are all cleanings getting done on time?
- Review photo verification — any missed verifications?
- Check supply inventory alerts — anything running low?
- Review cleaner ratings (if Turno Pro) — quality consistent?
- Action: Restock supplies, address any quality issues with cleaning team

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Turno dashboard]**

**Block 3: Pricing Performance (5 minutes)**
- Open PriceLabs dashboard — review occupancy forecast vs actual
- Compare this month's ADR to last month — trending up or down?
- Check orphan day fill rate — how many gaps were filled?
- Review any rate overrides you made — were they right?
- Action: Adjust seasonal rules if market conditions changed

**[SCREEN RECORDING: PriceLabs performance dashboard]**

**Block 4: Email & Marketing (5 minutes)**
- Check email platform metrics — open rates, click rates for each sequence
- Review request sequence — what's the review rate this month?
- Return guest emails — any bookings attributed to the sequence?
- Referral program — any new referrals this month?
- Action: Update subject lines on low-performing emails

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Email platform analytics]**

**Block 5: Direct Booking & Traffic (5 minutes)**
- Open Google Analytics — website traffic vs last month
- Check conversion rate — up or down?
- Check direct booking percentage — tracking toward your annual target?
- Review Google Ads performance — cost per booking, ROAS
- Action: Increase budget on winning channels, pause losers

**[SCREEN RECORDING: GA4 dashboard]**

**Block 6: Revenue Per Night (5 minutes)**
- Update your Revenue Per Night Calculator with this month's actual data
- Compare to your 12-month target — on track?
- Identify the biggest gap: occupancy, ADR, or commission savings?
- Set next month's focus area
- Choose ONE automation to improve or add this month

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Revenue Per Night Calculator update]**

Total time: 30 minutes. Do this on the first Sunday of every month. Put it on your calendar right now."
