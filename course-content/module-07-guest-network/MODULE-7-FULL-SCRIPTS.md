# LESSON 3: Repeat Bookings & Referral Programs (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"A guest who books a second time is worth 5x a first-timer: zero acquisition cost, higher average spend, better reviews, more likely to refer. This lesson builds the system that turns first-timers into regulars."

### LOYALTY TIERS [SLIDES]
"Create a simple loyalty structure:

**Silver (2 stays):** 10% off all direct bookings + early access to peak season dates
**Gold (4 stays):** 15% off + early access + complimentary mid-stay cleaning
**Platinum (6+ stays):** 20% off + early access + mid-stay cleaning + complimentary local experience (personal chef dinner, wine tour, etc.)

Communicate tiers in your post-stay emails: 'You're now a Silver member! Your next direct booking is 10% off — forever.'

This costs you very little (the discounts are on DIRECT bookings where you're already saving 15% in commissions) and dramatically increases repeat booking rates."

### REFERRAL PROGRAM MECHANICS [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]
"The 'Give $50, Get $50' program:

**How it works:**
- Every past guest gets a unique referral link (generated by your email platform or a tool like ReferralCandy)
- When someone books using that link, the new guest gets $50 off
- The referrer gets $50 credit toward their next stay
- Both are notified automatically

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Setting up referral tracking]**

**Promotion cadence:**
- Day 30 post-stay email includes the referral ask (already built)
- Monthly newsletter reminds guests about the program
- Physical referral card at checkout (Canva template provided — QR code + referral link)
- Digital guidebook mentions the referral program
- Every satisfied guest gets asked: 'Know someone who'd love this?'

**Target:** 10-20% of bookings from referrals within 18 months. At an average booking of $800, every referral saves you $120 in acquisition cost (platform commission) while only costing you $100 in credits ($50 + $50)."

# LESSON 4: Review Generation Engine (8-10 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Reviews are social proof AND algorithmic fuel. More 5-star reviews = higher Airbnb ranking = more bookings. A 4.9 average with 50+ reviews is the threshold for Guest Favorite status. This lesson turns review collection into an automated machine with a 40%+ response rate."

### THE REVIEW SYSTEM [SLIDES]
"You already built the review request sequence in Module 2 (3 emails at Day 1, Day 3, Day 7). Now let's optimize it and add the SMS layer.

**Optimization 1: Timing**
The first review request should hit 24 hours after checkout — not immediately. Give them time to get home, unpack, and reflect on the experience. A request sent during their drive home gets deleted.

**Optimization 2: Platform-specific links**
Create separate review links for each platform:
- Airbnb review link (found in your host dashboard)
- Google review link (found in Google Business Profile)
- Your direct site (testimonial submission form)

In the email, prioritize: If they booked on Airbnb, send the Airbnb review link. If they booked direct, send the Google review link. Your email segmentation handles this automatically.

**Optimization 3: The SMS backup**
If no review after 72 hours, send an SMS:
'Hi [Name]! Thanks again for staying with us. If you have 2 min, a quick review means the world: [link]. Thanks! - [Your name]'

SMS has a 95% open rate. This alone can push your review rate from 30% to 40%+.

**Optimization 4: The incentive**
In the Day 7 email (final ask): 'As a thank you for sharing your feedback, enjoy 10% off your next direct booking.' This is NOT paying for reviews (which violates platform terms). It's a thank-you for taking the time — the discount applies regardless of what they write."

### HANDLING NEGATIVE REVIEWS [CANON 70D + SLIDES]
"Not every review will be 5 stars. Here's how to handle it:

**4-star review:** Don't panic. Respond publicly: 'Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We're glad you enjoyed [positive thing they mentioned]. We've already [specific action you took to address their concern]. We'd love to host you again!'

**3-star review:** Respond publicly with empathy: 'We're sorry your experience didn't meet expectations, [Name]. We take your feedback seriously and have [specific fix]. We'd love the chance to make it right on a future stay — please reach out directly.'

**1-2 star review:** Respond publicly AND reach out privately. Offer a partial refund or future discount if the complaint is legitimate. Your response is for FUTURE guests reading the review, not just the reviewer.

**The key:** NEVER argue. NEVER be defensive. Every response demonstrates to future guests that you care and take action. A thoughtful response to a negative review can actually INCREASE bookings."
