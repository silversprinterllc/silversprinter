# LESSON 1: Local Business Partnerships (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"The easiest partnerships to land are in your backyard. Local restaurants, tour operators, wineries, golf courses, and activity providers all want one thing: customers. You have exactly what they want — a steady stream of travelers looking for things to do. This lesson shows you how to turn that into revenue."

### THE PARTNERSHIP PITCH [SLIDES]
"The pitch is simple: 'I send you customers. You give my guests a perk. We both win.'

Three partnership structures, from simplest to most profitable:

**Structure 1: Discount Exchange (Free)**
'I'll recommend your restaurant in my digital guidebook and guest emails. In exchange, you offer my guests 10-15% off.' Cost to you: zero. Cost to them: a small discount on new customers they wouldn't have had otherwise. Win-win.

**Structure 2: Referral Commission (Revenue Share)**
'I send you customers through a tracked link or code. You pay me 10-15% commission on each referral.' This requires a business that can track referrals — tour operators, boat charters, personal chefs do this naturally. Restaurants usually can't.

**Structure 3: Flat Monthly Sponsorship (Premium)**
'I feature your business prominently in my welcome guide, digital guidebook, and email sequences — reaching 20-50 guests per month. Sponsorship: $100-$300/month.' This works once you have significant guest volume.

**Start with Structure 1** — it's free, fast, and gets your foot in the door. Upgrade to 2 or 3 once the relationship proves value."

### IDENTIFYING PARTNERS [SLIDES + CANON]
"Who to approach — the 10 categories:

1. **Restaurants** (2-3 partners) — one upscale, one casual, one breakfast spot
2. **Tour operators** — boat tours, kayak rentals, fishing charters, snorkeling
3. **Wineries / breweries** — if your market has them, these are gold for couples
4. **Golf courses** — if your market is a golf destination
5. **Spa / wellness** — massage therapists, yoga instructors, wellness centers
6. **Water sports** — paddleboard, jet ski, surf lessons, sailing
7. **Bike / scooter rental** — especially in beach or urban markets
8. **Personal chef** — this is a premium upsell (we cover it more in Module 9)
9. **Photography** — family beach photos, couples sunset sessions
10. **Grocery delivery** — partner with a local service or use Instacart with a service fee markup

**How to approach them:**

Walk in, eat there, or call. Introduce yourself as a local vacation rental owner who wants to recommend their business to guests. Most local businesses will say yes immediately — you're offering them free marketing.

**Email template for follow-up:**

'Hi [Owner/Manager Name],

I'm [Your Name], owner of [Property Name] — a vacation rental in [area] that hosts [X] guests per month. I'd love to recommend [Business Name] to our guests as a top local pick.

Would you be open to offering our guests a [10-15% discount / special perk] that I can feature in our welcome guide and guest communications? Happy to display your menu/brochure at the property too.

Let me know — would love to chat over coffee.

[Your Name]
[Property Name]
[Phone]'

**That email has a 60-80% positive response rate** because you're offering something for almost nothing."

### IMPLEMENTATION [SLIDES]
"Once partnerships are confirmed:

1. Add to your **digital guidebook** (TouchStay/Hostfully) with description, photos, and discount code
2. Add to your **pre-arrival email** as a local recommendation
3. Create a **'Local Partners' page** on your direct booking site
4. Print a **local partnerships card** for the property (Canva template provided)
5. Track referrals in your **Partner Revenue Tracker** spreadsheet

**Revenue target: $100-$500/month per property** from local partnerships alone. This grows as your guest volume grows and as you upgrade from discount exchange to commission/sponsorship structures."

### CLOSE [CANON 70D]
"**Homework:**
1. List 10 local businesses in the 10 categories above
2. Visit or contact 5 this week with the partnership pitch
3. Set up at least 3 discount exchange partnerships
4. Add partners to your digital guidebook and pre-arrival email
5. Print a local partnerships card for your property"

# LESSON 2: Brand & Product Partnerships (8-10 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Your property is a showroom. Hundreds of guests per year sleep on your mattress, use your coffee maker, shower with your products, and sit on your furniture. Brands will give you premium products for free — or pay you — to be in that showroom. Let me show you how."

### BRAND PARTNERSHIP OPPORTUNITIES [SLIDES]
"Industries that actively partner with STR properties:

**Coffee/Beverage:** Local roasters, specialty tea brands, kombucha companies — they provide product, you display and recommend. Guest asks 'where can I buy this coffee?' → you provide the purchase link (with your affiliate cut).

**Bath/Wellness:** Luxury soap, shampoo, candles, essential oils. Brands love having their products in 'real' settings rather than staged photos. You get free restocking. They get organic social proof when guests post photos.

**Bedding/Linens:** Mattress and linen companies have hospitality partnership programs. Some provide mattresses at 50-80% discount or free in exchange for featuring their brand card on the nightstand.

**Furniture/Decor:** Emerging brands will furnish rooms in exchange for photo rights and guest exposure. This is most common with mid-range design brands trying to build portfolio.

**Minoan Experience:** This is a platform that turns your property into a shoppable space. Guests can scan QR codes on furniture, linens, and decor to purchase the same products for their own home. You earn a commission on every purchase. Set up at minoan.com.

**Outreach template:**
'Hi [Brand],

I own [Property Name], a [type] vacation rental in [City] that hosts [X]+ guests per year. I'd love to feature your [product type] in our property — guests consistently ask about the [products] we use.

In exchange for [product supply / discounted pricing / partnership], I'd be happy to:
- Display your brand card at the property
- Feature your products in our digital guidebook with purchase links
- Tag your brand in social media content featuring the property
- Share guest engagement data quarterly

Would you be open to exploring a partnership?

[Your Name] | [Property Name] | [Website]'"

### CLOSE [CANON 70D]
"**Homework:**
1. Identify 3 brands in your property that guests comment on or ask about
2. Research if those brands have hospitality/ambassador programs
3. Send 3 brand outreach emails using the template
4. Sign up for Minoan Experience and explore the shoppable stays model
5. Add any existing product recommendations to your digital guidebook with purchase links"

# LESSON 3: B2B & Corporate Partnerships (8-10 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Beyond individual guests and consumer brands, there's a B2B layer most hosts never tap. Corporate retreats, photo shoot locations, event venues, and real estate partnerships can generate $5,000-$20,000+ per year in non-booking revenue."

### B2B OPPORTUNITIES [SLIDES]
"**Corporate Retreats**
Companies need offsite locations for team retreats, strategy sessions, and team building. Your 4+ bedroom property with a large living area and outdoor space is exactly what a 6-12 person team needs.

Package it: 2-3 night corporate retreat package including property + welcome setup + local restaurant dinner reservation + team activity (escape room, cooking class, fishing charter). Price at 20-30% above standard nightly rate. Post on your direct site as a dedicated 'Corporate Retreats' page.

**Photo/Video Shoot Location**
Photographers, videographers, and brands need shoot locations. Charge a venue fee ($500-$2,000/day depending on property quality) separate from any overnight stay.

List on: Peerspace, Giggster, or local photographer Facebook groups. Even 2-3 shoots per year at $1,000 each adds $3,000 in zero-effort revenue.

**Wedding/Event Venue**
If your property and local regulations allow it, small events (10-30 people) like rehearsal dinners, intimate weddings, or birthday parties can command $2,000-$5,000 per event. Requires liability insurance — check with your provider.

**Real Estate Partnerships (Your Barefoot Advantage)**
This is where Module 8 connects to Module 10. Partner with real estate agents who sell properties to investors. When their buyer needs STR setup help → they refer to your course/consulting. When your course students want to buy property → you refer to Barefoot Realty.

Dual revenue: referral fees from agents + brokerage commissions from acquisitions."

### TRACKING & MEASUREMENT [SLIDES]
"**Partner Revenue Tracker** — spreadsheet template provided:
- Partner name, category, deal structure
- Monthly referrals sent, revenue generated, commission earned
- Quarterly review: keep, renegotiate, or replace

Review partners quarterly. Drop anyone not generating value. Double down on top performers."

### MODULE 8 WRAP-UP [CANON 70D]
"**Spoke 8 — BUILT.** You have local partnerships, brand partnerships in progress, and a B2B strategy for corporate retreats, shoots, and real estate referrals.

**Revenue target:** $200-$800/month from all partnership revenue combined. This grows significantly as guest volume increases and partnerships mature."
