# LESSON 1: Content Strategy & Pillars (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Every booking starts with someone discovering your property. And in 2025-2026, discovery happens on social media before it happens on Airbnb. A single TikTok property tour can generate more bookings than a month of paid advertising. This lesson builds your content strategy from scratch — what to create, where to post, and how it all connects back to bookings."

### THE CONTENT-TO-BOOKING FUNNEL [SLIDES]
"Content doesn't directly sell bookings. It creates a funnel:

**Discovery → Trust → Visit Site → Book**

- A traveler sees your Reel of a sunset from your deck. That's discovery.
- They visit your profile, see 20 posts showing the property, local attractions, happy guests. That's trust.
- They click the link in your bio to your direct booking site. That's the visit.
- They check dates and book. That's the conversion.

Every piece of content you create serves one of these four stages. Most hosts skip straight to 'book my rental!' posts. That's like proposing marriage on the first date."

### THE 4 CONTENT PILLARS [SLIDES]
"Every successful content account runs on 3-5 pillars — recurring themes that keep your feed cohesive and your audience growing.

**Pillar 1: Property Tours (40% of content)**
Walk-throughs, room reveals, amenity showcases, before/after staging, seasonal decor changes. These are your highest-performing posts because people LOVE looking at beautiful spaces.

**Pillar 2: Local Guides (30% of content)**
'Best restaurants in [City],' 'Hidden beaches only locals know,' '5 things to do on a rainy day.' This content attracts travelers who are PLANNING a trip to your area — before they've even booked a place to stay.

**Pillar 3: Behind-the-Scenes (15% of content)**
Turnover day timelapse, restocking the kitchen, setting up a guest welcome basket, your morning routine as a host. This humanizes your brand and builds trust.

**Pillar 4: Guest Experiences (15% of content)**
Guest photos (with permission), reviews highlighted as graphics, 'POV: you booked [Property Name]' lifestyle content. Social proof in visual form."

"Open the 30-Day Content Calendar template in your course resources.

The structure: post 4-5 times per week across your primary platform (Instagram or TikTok), repurpose to 2-3 secondary platforms.

Fill in your first month right now. It takes 15 minutes."

### WHICH PLATFORMS MATTER [SLIDES]
"You don't need to be on every platform. Pick ONE primary and 2-3 secondary:

| Property Type | Primary Platform | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Luxury / design-forward | Instagram | Pinterest, TikTok |
| Family / fun amenities | TikTok | Instagram, YouTube |
| Adventure / outdoor | TikTok | YouTube, Pinterest |
| Urban / business | Instagram | LinkedIn, Pinterest |
| Any | Pinterest (long game) | — |

Pinterest deserves special mention: pins have a 6-12 month lifespan vs 24-48 hours for Instagram posts. It's a search engine, not a social network. Every pin is a fishing line in the water that catches travelers for months."

# LESSON 2: Short-Form Video Production (12-14 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Short-form video is the highest-reach, lowest-cost content format available to you. One 30-second TikTok can get 100,000 views. An Instagram Reel can drive more traffic to your direct site than a month of Google Ads. In this lesson, I'm showing you the 5 video formats that work for STR, how to film them on your phone, and how to edit them in CapCut in under 10 minutes."

### THE 5 VIDEO FORMATS [CANON 70D + SLIDES]
"These are the 5 formats that consistently perform for vacation rental accounts:

**Format 1: The Property Walkthrough**
Walk through your property front door to back, showing every room. Use trending audio. Add text overlays calling out features. 30-60 seconds. This is your bread and butter — post one every week from a slightly different angle or featuring a different detail.

**Format 2: 'POV: You Booked [City]'**
First-person perspective showing the guest experience — pulling up to the house, opening the door, walking to the pool, watching sunset from the deck. Aspirational, lifestyle-focused. Use dreamy/relaxing trending audio.

**Format 3: Local Hidden Gems**
'3 spots in [City] tourists don't know about.' Quick cuts of a secret beach, a local restaurant, a scenic overlook. Positions you as the local expert — and attracts travelers planning a trip to your area.

**Format 4: Transformation / Before-After**
Staging a room, decorating for a holiday, turnover timelapse (messy → spotless in 30 seconds). These get massive engagement because people love transformations.

**Format 5: Day-in-the-Life Guest Experience**
'A day at [Property Name]' — morning coffee on the deck, beach walk, poolside lunch, sunset dinner. This is the 'what it FEELS like to stay here' video. Highest conversion to booking."

### FILMING BASICS [CANON 70D]
"Equipment: your phone. That's it.

- Shoot in 4K or 1080p (Settings > Camera > Record Video > 4K)
- Shoot VERTICAL (9:16) for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
- Natural light is your best friend — film during golden hour (sunrise/sunset) or bright midday
- Stabilize: hold your phone with two hands, tuck elbows to your body, walk slowly
- Clean your lens. Seriously. A smudged lens makes a $1,500/night property look like a hostel.
- Film 3-5x more than you need. A 30-second video needs 2-3 minutes of raw footage.

**The Hook Rule:** You have 2 seconds to stop someone from scrolling. Start with your BEST shot — the pool, the view, the dramatic entrance. Not the driveway. Not your face. The money shot."

### EDITING IN CAPCUT [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]
"Let me show you how to edit a property walkthrough in CapCut. This takes under 10 minutes.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: CapCut app or desktop]**

Step 1: Import your raw clips
Step 2: Trim each clip to 3-5 seconds (fast cuts = higher retention)
Step 3: Add trending audio (CapCut has a built-in trending music library)
Step 4: Add text overlays — call out each room/feature as it appears
Step 5: Add auto-captions (CapCut does this in one click)
Step 6: Add a hook text in the first frame: '3BR beach house you need to see' or 'This Destin rental has a SECRET pool'
Step 7: Export and post

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Live edit of a sample video, start to finish]**

The entire edit took 8 minutes. Do this twice a week and you have more content than 99% of hosts."

### POSTING STRATEGY [SLIDES]
"**Frequency:**
- TikTok: 4-5x per week (the algorithm rewards volume)
- Instagram Reels: 3-4x per week
- YouTube Shorts: 2-3x per week
- All three: repurpose the SAME video — film once, post everywhere

**Best times to post** (vacation rental audience):
- Tuesday-Thursday: 7-9pm (people planning trips after work)
- Saturday: 10am-12pm (weekend trip dreaming)
- Sunday: 7-9pm (planning the upcoming week's getaway)

**Hashtags (Instagram):** Use 5-10 relevant hashtags, mix of broad and niche:
Broad: #vacationrental #airbnb #travelgram
Niche: #destinflorida #beachhouserental #gulfcoastvacation #[yourcity]rental

**Hashtags (TikTok):** Fewer hashtags, more specific:
#destin #beachhouse #vacationrental #airbnbfinds #traveltiktok

**Every post caption ends with:** 'Book direct at [URL] and save — link in bio'"

# LESSON 3: Long-Form Content & Pinterest (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Short-form video gets you discovered. Long-form content keeps you found. A YouTube video about 'Best things to do in [your city]' can drive traffic for YEARS. A Pinterest pin has a 6-12 month lifespan. These are assets that compound. Let's build them."

### YOUTUBE STRATEGY [CANON 70D + SLIDES]
"You don't need to be a YouTuber. You need 3-5 strategic videos that target searches travelers actually make.

**Video 1: Full Property Tour (3-5 min)**
Complete walkthrough with narration. This is for travelers who are seriously considering booking — they want to see EVERYTHING. Post on YouTube AND embed on your direct booking site.

**Video 2: '[City] Vacation Guide' (8-12 min)**
Everything a first-time visitor needs to know: best time to visit, best areas to stay, top restaurants, activities, beaches, day trips. Target keyword: '[city] vacation guide.' This video captures travelers before they've even decided where to stay.

**Video 3: 'Best Things to Do in [City]' (8-12 min)**
Top 10 activities, attractions, and experiences. Target keyword: 'things to do in [city].' This is the highest-volume search travelers make.

Film these 3 videos in one weekend. They live on YouTube forever and drive traffic to your direct booking site through your video description links."

# LESSON 4: User-Generated Content & Measurement (8-10 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"The most trusted content about your property doesn't come from you — it comes from your guests. User-generated content converts 4x better than brand content because it's authentic. This lesson shows you how to systematically encourage, collect, and repurpose guest content — plus how to measure what's actually driving bookings."

### CREATING UGC OPPORTUNITIES [CANON 70D + SLIDES]
"Guests won't create content by accident. You need to engineer moments worth sharing.

**Physical triggers in your property:**
- A branded welcome sign they photograph on arrival
- A 'photo wall' or neon sign with your property name (Instagram bait)
- A sunset viewing deck with chairs perfectly positioned for photos
- A unique design element: statement wall, hanging chair, rooftop hammock
- A chalkboard or letter board: 'The [Last Name] Family — [City] 2026'

Cost: $50-$200 for a few strategic installations that generate free content for years.

**Digital triggers:**
- Post-arrival text: 'Settling in? Tag us @[handle] in your photos and we'll repost our favorites!'
- Check-in card on the counter: 'Share your stay! #[PropertyName] — we repost our favorites and offer 10% off your next stay'
- Pre-arrival email mentions the photo spots
- Post-stay email asks permission to repost their photos

**The incentive:** '10% off your next direct booking when you post and tag us.' Small cost, massive return — you get free content AND a reason for them to book direct next time."

### REPURPOSING UGC [SLIDES]
"One guest photo can become 5 pieces of content:
1. Repost on Instagram Stories (tag the guest)
2. Add to a 'Guest Favorites' carousel post
3. Feature on your direct booking website testimonials section
4. Include in your email newsletter
5. Use as a Pinterest pin

Always credit the guest. Always ask permission before reposting to your main feed (Stories are generally fine with a tag)."

### MEASUREMENT DASHBOARD [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]
"**[SCREEN RECORDING: Content tracking spreadsheet]**

Track monthly:
| Metric | Source | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Total posts published | Manual count | 16-20/month |
| Total video views | Platform analytics | Growing month over month |
| Profile visits | Platform analytics | Growing |
| Website clicks from social | GA4 (UTM tracking) | 50+/month |
| Direct bookings from social | GA4 (source tracking) | 2+/month |
| Email signups from content | Email platform | 20+/month |
| UGC pieces collected | Manual count | 5+/month |

The metric that matters most: **website clicks from social.** If content drives traffic to your direct site, the email funnels and booking engine do the rest.

Set up UTM links for each platform:
- Instagram bio: `yoursite.com?utm_source=instagram`
- TikTok bio: `yoursite.com?utm_source=tiktok`
- Pinterest: `yoursite.com?utm_source=pinterest`
- YouTube descriptions: `yoursite.com?utm_source=youtube`

This lets GA4 show you exactly which platform is driving traffic."
