## RECORDING ORDER (Recommended)

### Session 1: Talking Head Segments (2-3 hours)
Record all on-camera segments back-to-back in one session. Same outfit, same lighting, same energy.

**What to record:**
1. Lesson 1 — Full script (8-10 min on camera)
2. Lesson 2 — Hook + Three Metrics explanation (0:00-4:00)
3. Lesson 2 — Close (9:00-10:30)
4. Lesson 3 — Hook + Three Tiers + Moving Up + Close (entire lesson minus screen recording)
5. Lesson 4 — Roadmap section (4:00-10:00) + Close

**Tips:**
- Record HOOK segments with highest energy — re-take until the first 10 seconds are punchy
- Change camera angle slightly between lessons (zoom in/out) for visual variety
- Use a teleprompter app if needed (BigVu or PromptSmart are good free options)
- Record at least 2 takes of each hook — pick the best in editing

### Session 2: Screen Recordings (1-2 hours)
Record all screen share segments separately. These are easier and can be done in a quieter setting.

**What to record:**
1. AirDNA walkthrough for Lesson 2 (market data pull)
2. Revenue Per Night Calculator walkthrough for Lesson 2 (Sections 1-5)
3. AirDNA competitive analysis for Lesson 3 (competitor browse)
4. Revenue Per Night Calculator deep-dive for Lesson 4 (Sections 6-8)
5. Roadmap template fill-in for Lesson 4

**Tips:**
- Use OBS Studio (free) or Loom (free tier) for screen recording
- Record at 1920x1080 resolution minimum
- Use a clean browser with no personal bookmarks or distracting tabs
- Zoom into the spreadsheet so text is readable on mobile (most students watch on phone)
- Talk through what you're clicking — narrate every action

### Session 3: Assembly & Post-Production (3-4 hours)
Combine talking head + screen recordings + slides + B-roll in your editor.

## EQUIPMENT RECOMMENDATIONS

### Minimum Viable Setup ($0-$100)
- **Camera:** iPhone 12+ or any modern smartphone (4K capable)
- **Audio:** Wired lavalier mic ($15-$25 on Amazon — Boya BY-M1 or similar)
- **Lighting:** Natural window light (face the window, camera between you and window)
- **Tripod:** Phone tripod with adjustable height ($15-$25)
- **Background:** Clean wall, bookshelf, or blurred background
- **Teleprompter:** BigVu app (free tier) or tape script pages near the lens

### Upgraded Setup ($200-$500)
- **Camera:** Same phone OR webcam (Logitech C920/C922)
- **Audio:** Blue Yeti USB mic ($80) or Rode NT-USB Mini ($100)
- **Lighting:** Ring light ($30-$50) OR two softbox lights ($60-$100)
- **Background:** Branded backdrop or styled bookshelf
- **Teleprompter:** iPad with teleprompter app mounted near lens

### Screen Recording
- **OBS Studio** (free) — best for screen recording with face cam overlay
- **Loom** (free tier) — easiest for quick screen recordings
- **Descript** (paid) — records and edits with AI transcript editing

## AI VIDEO PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Record Raw Footage
- Talking head segments (phone or webcam)
- Screen recordings (OBS or Loom)
- Total raw footage: ~60-90 minutes for all 4 lessons

### Step 2: AI Audio Cleanup (Descript — $24/mo)
- Import all footage into Descript
- Auto-transcribe
- Remove filler words ("um," "uh," "like") with one click
- Remove long pauses
- Adjust audio levels and reduce background noise
- Export cleaned footage

### Step 3: AI-Enhanced Editing (CapCut Pro — $7.99/mo or HeyGen)
For talking head segments:
- Add branded lower thirds (name, lesson title)
- Add animated captions/subtitles (CapCut auto-captions)
- Add transition effects between sections
- Add zoom-in effects on key statements for emphasis

For screen recordings:
- Add zoom/highlight effects on areas you're clicking
- Add cursor highlighting
- Add step number overlays ("Step 1," "Step 2")

### Step 4: Slides & Diagrams (Canva Pro — $13/mo or Gamma)
- Build all slides using course brand template (navy/gold/cream)
- Animate the hub-and-spoke diagram (Canva Animate)
- Export as video clips (MP4) to layer into the edit
- Alternative: Use Gamma.app for AI-generated presentations that animate automatically

### Step 5: B-Roll Enhancement (optional)
- Source stock B-roll from Pexels (free) or Storyblocks ($15/mo)
- Search: "luxury vacation rental," "beach house interior," "booking app screen," "guest arriving"
- Layer B-roll over talking head during explanatory sections to break up visual monotony

### Step 6: Final Assembly
- Combine all elements in CapCut Pro, DaVinci Resolve (free), or Descript
- Add branded intro (3 seconds — logo + lesson title)
- Add branded outro (5 seconds — "Up Next" card + RevenuePerNight logo)
- Add background music (subtle, low volume — use Epidemic Sound or Artlist for licensed tracks)
- Export at 1080p minimum, 4K if possible

### Step 7: Upload to Course Platform
- Teachable, Kajabi, Thinkific, or Skool
- Set up Module 0 with 4 lessons in order
- Attach the Revenue Per Night Calculator as a downloadable resource
- Attach the Foundation Completion Checklist
