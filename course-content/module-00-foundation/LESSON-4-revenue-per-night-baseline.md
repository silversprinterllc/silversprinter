### COMPLETING THE CALCULATOR (0:30 - 4:00)

"Open your Revenue Per Night Calculator. You should have Sections 1 through 5 filled in from Lesson 2. Now we're going to complete the final sections.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Calculator spreadsheet]**

**Section 6: Expense Baseline**

Revenue means nothing without understanding costs. Fill in your monthly expenses:

- Mortgage or rent: $___
- Insurance: $___
- Utilities: $___
- Cleaning (per turnover): $___ x estimated turnovers/month
- Supplies and consumables: $___
- Software tools (PMS, pricing, etc.): $___
- Platform fees/commissions: $___
- Maintenance reserve (5-10% of revenue): $___

The calculator totals your annual expenses and shows you:
- **Gross Revenue Per Night** (before expenses)
- **Net Revenue Per Night** (after expenses)
- **Profit margin percentage**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Numbers populating]**

For our example property:
- Gross Revenue: $47,000 ($129/available night)
- Total Expenses: $28,200
- Net Revenue: $18,800 ($51.50/available night)
- Profit Margin: 40%

If your profit margin is below 35%, you either have a revenue problem or an expense problem. The modules will help you fix the revenue side. The expense side is simpler — audit every line item and cut what doesn't generate bookings or improve guest experience.

**Section 7: Spoke Assessment**

This is where the Hub-and-Spoke model meets your numbers. For each of the 8 spokes, rate yourself:

- **Active** — This spoke is built and generating results
- **Partial** — Started but not optimized
- **Missing** — Not built at all

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Filling in spoke assessment]**

Most hosts who take this assessment look something like:

| Spoke | Status |
|-------|--------|
| Distribution Platforms | Partial (Airbnb + maybe VRBO) |
| Direct Booking | Missing |
| Dynamic Pricing | Missing |
| Automation | Missing or Partial |
| Content & Social | Missing |
| Creator Network | Missing |
| Guest Network | Missing |
| Curated Experiences | Missing |

If you have 2 out of 8 spokes active, you're operating at 25% capacity. The upside is massive — and now you can see exactly where it is.

**Section 8: Target Setting and Gap Analysis**

Now set your 12-month targets:

- Target Occupancy Rate: ___% (aim for top 25% in your market)
- Target ADR: $___ (based on your premium positioning analysis from Lesson 3)
- Target Revenue Per Night: $___ (auto-calculated)
- Target Annual Gross Revenue: $___ (auto-calculated)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Target vs current comparison]**

The calculator now shows your **Revenue Gap** — the difference between where you are and where you want to be.

For our example:
- Current annual revenue: $47,000
- Target annual revenue: $68,700
- **Revenue Gap: $21,700**

That's $21,700 per year — or $1,808 per month — hiding in your property. The next 9 modules show you exactly how to capture it."

### THE 12-MONTH ROADMAP (4:00 - 8:00)

"Now let's build your implementation roadmap. This is the plan that turns the program from information into income.

**[SLIDE: The Spoke Priority Matrix]**

Not all spokes are equal. Some produce revenue faster. Some have higher long-term value. Here's how I recommend prioritizing them:

**[SLIDE: Priority tiers appearing]**

**Tier 1 — Build Immediately (Months 1-3): Highest ROI, fastest results**
- Module 3: Dynamic Pricing — Can increase revenue 15-40% within DAYS of implementation. Lowest effort, highest immediate return.
- Module 1: Distribution Platforms — Adding 2-3 platforms takes a few days and increases visibility immediately.
- Module 4: Automation — Gets your time back so you can focus on building the other spokes.

**Tier 2 — Build Next (Months 2-5): Revenue infrastructure**
- Module 2: Direct Booking Engine — Takes 2-3 weeks to build but starts saving commissions and capturing guest data from day one.
- Module 7: Guest Network — Start capturing emails NOW so you have a list to market to.

**Tier 3 — Growth Accelerators (Months 4-8): Scale and brand**
- Module 5: Content & Social — Content compounds over time. Start as soon as Tier 1 and 2 are running.
- Module 6: Creator Network — Once your property is optimized and your content is flowing, bring in creators.
- Module 9: Curated Experiences — Add revenue layers on top of the booking engine.

**Tier 4 — Long Game (Months 6-12+): Wealth building**
- Module 8: Product Placement & Sponsors — Once you have traffic and guest volume, brands will come.
- Module 10: Barefoot Advantage — Property acquisition when you're ready to scale from operator to investor.

**[SLIDE: 12-Month Timeline]**

Here's what a typical implementation timeline looks like:

**Month 1:** Dynamic pricing installed. 3 new platforms listed. PMS and channel manager configured. Operations automated. (Modules 0, 1, 3, 4)

**Month 2:** Direct booking website live. StayFi installed. Guest data capture started. First email sequences running. (Module 2)

**Month 3:** Content strategy launched. First TikToks and Reels posted. Blog posts published. Pinterest started. (Module 5)

**Month 4:** First influencer stay. Referral program launched. Guest retention sequences active. (Modules 6, 7)

**Month 5-6:** Direct bookings growing. Content compounding. Repeat guests booking. First experience partnerships. (Module 9)

**Month 7-8:** Optimization cycle. Monthly audits. A/B testing. Scaling what works. Cutting what doesn't.

**Month 9-12:** Full system running. 8 spokes active. Local business partnerships. Brand established. Considering property #2. (Modules 8, 10)

**[SLIDE: The Rule of One Spoke Per Week]**

Here's a practical rule: focus on building ONE spoke per 1-2 weeks. Don't try to do everything at once. One spoke, fully built, is worth more than five spokes half-built. Follow the priority order, complete each module's implementation checklist, and move to the next."
