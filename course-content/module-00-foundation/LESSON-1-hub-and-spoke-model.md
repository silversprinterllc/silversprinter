## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)

"Here's the biggest mistake short-term rental owners make — and it's costing you thousands every single month.

You listed your property on Airbnb. Maybe VRBO too. You set a nightly rate that felt right. And you wait for bookings.

That's not a business. That's a lottery ticket.

In this lesson, I'm going to show you the framework that changes everything — the Hub-and-Spoke Revenue Model. This is the system we use to turn single-platform listings into multi-channel hospitality businesses with predictable, growing revenue."

### THE PROBLEM (0:30 - 2:30)

"Let me paint the picture of where most hosts are right now.

**[SLIDE: 'The Single-Spoke Trap']**

You have one property. It's listed on Airbnb. Maybe you put it on VRBO too. And that's it. That's your entire demand engine — one or two platforms that you don't control.

Here's what that actually means:

**[SLIDE: Bullet points appearing one at a time]**

- Airbnb takes 15-20% of every booking in fees. On a property earning $50,000 a year, that's $7,500 to $10,000 — gone.
- You don't own your guest relationships. Airbnb does. When a guest books, Airbnb hides their email, their phone number, their contact info. They're Airbnb's customer, not yours.
- You're at the mercy of one algorithm. Airbnb changes their search ranking? Your bookings can drop 40% overnight. It happened to thousands of hosts in 2024 when they rolled out the Guest Favorite program.
- You have zero repeat booking system. A guest has an amazing stay, goes home, tells their friends — and when they want to come back, they go right back to Airbnb and you pay another 15% commission on a guest YOU already earned.
- You're competing on price because you look identical to every other listing. Same platform, same layout, same photos format. The only differentiator guests see is your nightly rate.

This is what I call the Single-Spoke Trap. You have one spoke — Airbnb — connecting your property to the market. One spoke breaks, your business breaks."

### THE FRAMEWORK (2:30 - 5:30)

"Now let me show you the alternative.

**[ANIMATION: Hub-and-spoke diagram building piece by piece]**

This is the Hub-and-Spoke Revenue Model. Your property — your physical rental — sits at the center. That's the hub. It never changes. It's the asset.

Around that hub, we build spokes. Each spoke is a demand channel — a system that drives bookings, revenue, or value to your property. And we're going to build not one, not two, but eight of them.

**[ANIMATION: Each spoke lights up as mentioned]**

**Spoke 1: Distribution Platforms.** Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, Expedia, TripAdvisor, and Google Vacation Rentals. Most hosts are on one or two. We put you on all six. More platforms, more visibility, more bookings.

**Spoke 2: Direct Booking Engine.** Your own website where guests book directly with you. No Airbnb. No commission. No middleman. You own the guest relationship, you keep 100% of the revenue.

**Spoke 3: Dynamic Pricing.** Data-driven pricing that adjusts your nightly rate in real-time based on demand, seasonality, local events, and competitor behavior. This single spoke can increase your revenue 15-40% without adding a single new booking.

**Spoke 4: Automation and Systems.** Guest messaging, cleaning coordination, pricing updates, review requests — all running on autopilot. This is how you go from spending 4 hours a day managing your property to under 2 hours a week.

**Spoke 5: Content and Social Media.** TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest — a content engine that makes your property visible to travelers who have never heard of you. One viral property tour can drive thousands of dollars in bookings.

**Spoke 6: Creator and Influencer Network.** Travel creators who promote your property to their audience in exchange for a complimentary stay. One creator with 10,000 followers can generate more bookings than months of paid advertising.

**Spoke 7: Guest Network.** Your past guests — turned into repeat visitors, referrers, and brand ambassadors. The cheapest, highest-converting booking source you'll ever have.

**Spoke 8: Curated Experiences.** Personal chefs, wine tours, boat charters, local guides — premium add-ons that increase your revenue per guest AND earn you affiliate commissions.

**[SLIDE: Full diagram with all 8 spokes illuminated]**

Eight spokes. Eight ways demand reaches your property. If one platform changes their algorithm, you have seven other channels still working. If one spoke underperforms, the others compensate. This is how you build a resilient, scalable business — not a fragile listing."

### WHY IT WORKS (5:30 - 7:00)

"Here's why this model is so powerful compared to what most hosts do.

**[SLIDE: 'Compounding Demand']**

Each spoke doesn't just add revenue — it multiplies the others.

When you build a direct booking site, your social media content has somewhere to send traffic. When you capture guest emails through WiFi, your email sequences drive repeat bookings to your direct site. When an influencer posts about your property, it drives both Airbnb bookings AND direct bookings. When you add curated experiences, your reviews go from 'nice house' to 'the best trip of our lives' — which boosts your ranking on every platform.

The spokes feed each other. It's a flywheel, not a collection of disconnected tactics.

**[SLIDE: Revenue comparison graphic]**

Let me give you real numbers. Take a property earning $50,000 a year on Airbnb alone.

- Add 3 more distribution platforms: +$8,000 from increased visibility
- Add dynamic pricing: +$7,500 from rate optimization
- Add direct bookings at 30% of total: Save $4,500 in commissions
- Add repeat guest system: +$5,000 from return bookings
- Add experience commissions: +$2,000 passive

That's the same property, same number of available nights, now generating $77,000 instead of $50,000. A 54% increase. That's what adding spokes does."

### WHAT YOU'LL BUILD IN THIS PROGRAM (7:00 - 8:30)

"Over the next 10 modules, we're going to build every single one of these spokes for YOUR property.

**[SLIDE: Module roadmap]**

We start with the foundation — that's this module. You'll calculate your current Revenue Per Night, benchmark your market, and understand exactly where you stand today.

Then we build the revenue spokes one by one. Distribution platforms. Direct booking engine. Dynamic pricing. Automation. Content. Influencers. Guest retention. Experiences.

Every module gives you:
- A clear lesson plan with step-by-step implementation
- The exact tools to use and how to configure them
- Templates and swipe files you can copy and paste
- A checklist so you know when the spoke is fully built

By the end, you won't have a listing on Airbnb. You'll have a hospitality business with predictable demand from eight channels.

Let's start by figuring out where you are right now. In the next lesson, we're going to calculate your three most important numbers — Occupancy Rate, ADR, and RevPAR. These are the metrics that tell you exactly how healthy your business is and where the biggest opportunities are hiding.

I'll see you there."

2. Use **Descript** to clean audio, remove filler words, auto-caption
3. Use **CapCut Pro** or **Runway** to add B-roll overlays, transitions, lower thirds

4. Add branded lower thirds, transitions, and intro/outro

2. AI-enhanced slides and diagrams for the educational middle section

4. Professional captions, transitions, and branding throughout
