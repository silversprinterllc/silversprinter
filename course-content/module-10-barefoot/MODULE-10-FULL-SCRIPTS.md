# LESSON 1: Ownership vs. Arbitrage — The Long Game (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Most STR courses teach you how to optimize a property you rent from someone else — Airbnb arbitrage, co-hosting, rental management. There's nothing wrong with that as a starting point. But the real wealth in this industry comes from OWNERSHIP. And the difference over 5-10 years isn't incremental — it's generational."

### THE ARBITRAGE TRAP [SLIDES]
"Let's compare two paths:

**Path A: Arbitrage Operator**
You lease a property for $3,000/month. You furnish it for $15,000. You list it on Airbnb. It generates $5,500/month in bookings. After rent, expenses, and platform fees, you net $800-$1,200/month.

Sounds good. Until:
- The landlord raises rent by $500/month → your margin collapses
- The landlord doesn't renew your lease → you lose the entire business overnight
- The city passes an STR regulation requiring owner-occupied permits → you're shut down
- You've built zero equity. Zero appreciation. Zero tax benefits. After 5 years you have: a business that depends entirely on someone else's property.

**Path B: Owner-Operator**
You purchase a $450,000 property with 25% down ($112,500). You furnish it for $15,000. Same $5,500/month in bookings. After mortgage, expenses, and platform fees, you net $600-$1,000/month in cash flow.

The cash flow is SIMILAR. But here's what arbitrage doesn't give you:

**[SLIDE: The 5 Wealth Multipliers — appearing one at a time]**

1. **Equity buildup:** Every mortgage payment builds ownership. After 5 years, you've paid down ~$30,000 in principal.
2. **Appreciation:** At 3% annual appreciation, that $450K property is worth $521,000 in 5 years. You gained $71,000 in value.
3. **Tax benefits:** Depreciation, mortgage interest deduction, operating expense write-offs. STR owners can often show a paper loss while pocketing real cash flow. (Consult your CPA — we're not tax advisors.)
4. **Leverage:** You control a $450,000 asset with $112,500 of your own money. That's 4:1 leverage. A 3% appreciation on the full value is an 11.8% return on YOUR invested capital.
5. **Control:** Nobody can raise your rent, cancel your lease, or kick you out. The property is yours.

**[SLIDE: 5-Year Comparison Table]**

| Metric | Arbitrage (5 years) | Ownership (5 years) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly cash flow | $800-$1,200 | $600-$1,000 |
| Total cash flow (5 yr) | $48,000-$72,000 | $36,000-$60,000 |
| Equity from mortgage paydown | $0 | ~$30,000 |
| Equity from appreciation | $0 | ~$71,000 |
| Tax savings (estimated) | Minimal | $15,000-$30,000 |
| Asset value at year 5 | $0 (you own nothing) | $521,000 |
| **Total 5-Year Wealth** | **$48K-$72K** | **$152K-$191K** |

The cash flow is similar. The WEALTH is 2-3x higher with ownership. And it compounds every year."

**Florida Keys**
- Key West / Islamorada — Premium, high ADR, limited supply (hard to build new), regulated"

### MARKET ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]
"**[SCREEN RECORDING: AirDNA MarketMinder]**

Here's how I evaluate a market using AirDNA. I look at 6 metrics:

**1. Average RevPAR** — Is the Revenue Per Night high enough to support a mortgage + expenses?
Target: $150+ RevPAR for a 3-bedroom property

**2. Occupancy Rate** — Is there consistent demand?
Target: 60%+ annual occupancy (higher is better, but 70-80% means pricing may be too low)

**3. Supply Growth** — Is the market getting flooded?
Target: Under 8% annual supply growth. Above 10% = red flag.

**4. Seasonality** — How extreme are the highs and lows?
Ideal: Less than 3x difference between peak and low month revenue. Extreme seasonality = risky cash flow.

**5. Regulation** — Are STR permits available and stable?
Check local county/city STR ordinances. Some Florida markets (Miami Beach, Key West) have heavy restrictions. Others (Destin, Orlando area) are STR-friendly.

**6. Entry Price** — Can you acquire a cash-flowing property at current prices?
Use the Deal Analysis Tool (Module 10 homework) to model specific properties.

### FINANCING OPTIONS [SLIDES]
"**[SLIDE: 4 Financing Paths]**

**1. Conventional Mortgage**
- 15-25% down, 6.5-8% interest (2025-2026 rates)
- Requires W-2 income or strong tax returns
- Best for: your first 1-4 investment properties
- Limitation: most lenders cap at 4-10 conventional mortgages

**2. DSCR Loan (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)**
- Qualifies based on PROPERTY income, not YOUR income
- 20-25% down, rates typically 0.5-1% higher than conventional
- Best for: investors who can't qualify conventionally or have maxed out conventional loans
- This is the primary tool for scaling to 5+ properties

**3. Portfolio / Private Lending**
- Local banks or private lenders who hold the loan in-house
- Terms are negotiable (down payment, rate, term)
- Best for: unique properties, quick closes, or investors with strong relationships
- Often required for 10+ property portfolios

**4. Partnerships / Syndication**
- Pool capital with other investors
- You bring the operating expertise (Hub-and-Spoke system), they bring capital
- Structure: LLC with defined equity splits and management fees
- Best for: scaling faster than your personal capital allows
