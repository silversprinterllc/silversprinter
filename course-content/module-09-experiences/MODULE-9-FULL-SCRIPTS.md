# LESSON 1: Airbnb Experiences (8-10 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Airbnb Experiences is a separate business inside Airbnb — and a marketing channel for your property that most hosts ignore. You host an activity, set your price, and Airbnb promotes it to millions of travelers. The kicker: your Experience listing links back to your property. It's a booking funnel disguised as a side hustle."

### HOW AIRBNB EXPERIENCES WORK [SLIDES]
"You design and host an activity — a food tour, a cooking class, a sunset kayak trip, a street art walk, a wine tasting. Anything where you (or a partner) can deliver an authentic local experience.

**Structure:**
- You set the price per guest ($30-$200 depending on the experience)
- You set the max group size (2-10 typically)
- You set the schedule (which days/times you offer it)
- Airbnb takes 20% commission
- You keep 80%

**Revenue potential:**
- $75/person × 6 guests × 3 sessions/week × 4 weeks = $5,400/month
- Even at 1 session/week: $1,800/month
- This is SEPARATE from your rental income

**The marketing play:**
Every Airbnb Experience has a host profile. Your host profile links to your property listings. Travelers who love your Experience discover your property — and book it. It's a demand funnel that Airbnb promotes for you.

**Requirements:**
- Demonstrate expertise or local connection (you don't need to be a certified guide)
- The experience must be unique and engaging (not just 'a walk around the city')
- Airbnb reviews and approves each Experience submission
- You can host yourself OR designate a co-host (partner with a local expert)"

### DESIGNING YOUR EXPERIENCE [CANON 70D]
"What works by market type:

| Market | Experience Ideas |
|---|---|
| Beach | Sunset paddleboard tour, shell collecting + crafting, beach bonfire cookout |
| Mountain | Sunrise hike to secret viewpoint, wild foraging walk, campfire storytelling |
| Wine Country | Private vineyard tour, wine blending class, picnic at a hidden vineyard |
| Urban | Street art walking tour, food truck crawl, rooftop photography session |
| Lake | Fishing 101, kayak nature tour, lakeside yoga |
| Any | Local cooking class in your property kitchen, photography walk, market-to-table dinner |

**The easiest path:** Partner with a local expert. A local chef hosts a cooking class IN your property kitchen. A local guide leads a nature hike. You handle the Airbnb listing and logistics. They deliver the experience. You split revenue 60/40 or 70/30.

This way you don't have to personally host every session."

### CLOSE [CANON 70D]
"**Homework:**
1. Brainstorm 3 experience ideas that match your market and property
2. Identify 1-2 local experts who could co-host
3. Create your Airbnb Experience listing (submit for review — takes 1-2 weeks for approval)
4. Set pricing, group size, and schedule"

# LESSON 2: Viator & Activity Affiliate Revenue (8-10 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"What if you could earn money from every activity your guests do — without hosting anything yourself? Viator pays you 8% commission on every tour, activity, or experience your guests book through your link. Set it up once, earn passively forever."

### VIATOR SETUP [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]
"**[SCREEN RECORDING: Viator partner signup]**

Step 1: Go to partnerresources.viator.com and click 'Join Now'
Step 2: Create your affiliate account — no qualifications needed, instant approval
Step 3: Search for activities in your market — Viator has 300,000+ experiences in 2,500 destinations
Step 4: Generate affiliate links for the top 10-15 activities your guests would love
Step 5: Embed these links in:
- Your digital guidebook (TouchStay/Hostfully)
- Your 'Things to Do' blog posts
- Your pre-arrival email
- Your direct booking website 'Local Experiences' page
- Your printed local guide at the property

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Searching for activities, generating affiliate links]**

**Commission structure:** 8% of booking value, 30-day cookie, weekly payouts.

**Revenue math:**
- 200 guests/year
- 50% book at least one activity through your links (if recommended well)
- Average activity: $80/person
- 100 bookings × $80 × 8% = **$640/year passive**
- With families/groups averaging 3 people: $80 × 3 × 100 × 8% = **$1,920/year**

It's not life-changing alone. But combined with all other spokes, it adds up — and requires ZERO ongoing effort after setup."

### CURATING YOUR TOP 10 LIST [SCREEN RECORDING]
"**[SCREEN RECORDING: Building a curated list]**

For your digital guidebook and emails, create a 'Top 10 Experiences Near [Property Name]' list:

1. [Highest-rated water activity] — 'Guests love this one'
2. [Best food tour or cooking class]
3. [Top-rated nature/outdoor activity]
4. [Family-friendly activity] — if you host families
5. [Romantic experience] — sunset cruise, wine tour
6. [Adventure activity] — jet ski, parasailing, zip line
7. [Cultural/historical tour]
8. [Half-day trip] — nearby town or attraction
9. [Full-day excursion] — day trip option
10. [Seasonal highlight] — changes quarterly

For each: 2-sentence description + 'Book here' with your affiliate link."

### CLOSE [CANON 70D]
"**Homework:**
1. Create your Viator affiliate account
2. Find and generate links for your Top 10 experiences
3. Add the Top 10 list to your digital guidebook
4. Add Viator links to your 'Things to Do' blog posts
5. Include experience recommendations in your pre-arrival email"

# LESSON 3: Premium Add-Ons & Experience Packages (10-12 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Viator gets you passive commissions. Now let's talk about PREMIUM add-ons — personal chefs, private wine tours, boat charters, and custom packages that command higher margins and transform your property from 'nice house' to 'unforgettable experience.'"

### PREMIUM EXPERIENCE PARTNERS [SLIDES]
"These are the partners that elevate your property into luxury territory:

**Personal Chef ($75-$200/person)**
Partner with 1-2 local personal chefs. They come to your property, cook a multi-course meal, clean up, and leave. You refer, they deliver. Your commission: 15-20% or a flat referral fee ($50-$100 per booking).

How to find them: Google '[city] personal chef for vacation rental,' check Thumbtack, or ask in local hospitality Facebook groups.

**Private Wine/Beer Tours ($50-$150/person)**
Partner with a local tour operator or create a custom arrangement with 2-3 wineries/breweries. Commission: 10-15%.

**Boat Charters ($200-$500/trip)**
Partner with local charter captains. Fishing trips, sunset cruises, island hopping. Commission: 10-15% or flat $50-$100 per referral.

**Photographer ($200-$500/session)**
Family beach photos, couples sunset sessions. Partner with a local photographer. Your commission: 15-20%.

**Grocery Pre-Stocking ($100-$200 + $50 service fee)**
Offer to stock the fridge before arrival. Partner with a local service or use Instacart. Your profit: the $50 service fee.

**Early Check-In / Late Check-Out ($50-$100)**
Offer when there's no same-day turnover. Pure profit."

# LESSON 4: Digital Guidebook as Revenue Engine (8-10 min)

## SCRIPT

### HOOK [CANON 70D]
"Your digital guidebook isn't a hospitality nicety — it's a storefront. Every guest opens it during their stay. Every page can generate revenue through affiliate links, partner recommendations, and upsell offers. Let's monetize it."

### GUIDEBOOK SETUP [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]
"**[SCREEN RECORDING: TouchStay or Hostfully guidebook editor]**

Structure your guidebook with revenue in mind:

**Section 1: Welcome & Check-In** (standard — no monetization needed)
**Section 2: House Guide** (WiFi, appliances, rules — standard)
**Section 3: Restaurants** → Each recommendation includes partner discount code or affiliate link
**Section 4: Activities & Adventures** → Viator affiliate links for each activity
**Section 5: Day Trips** → Viator links + driving directions
**Section 6: Premium Experiences** → Personal chef, boat charter, photography — direct booking links
**Section 7: Shopping & Services** → Grocery delivery, pharmacy, gas stations — practical
**Section 8: Emergency Contacts** → Standard
**Section 9: Book Direct & Save** → Link to your direct booking site: 'Loved your stay? Book direct next time and save 15%'
**Section 10: Refer a Friend** → Referral program link with 'Give $50, Get $50'

Sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, and 10 all generate revenue. That's 6 out of 10 sections working for you.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Adding affiliate links and partner sections to guidebook]**

Update the guidebook seasonally — swap summer activities for winter ones, add new restaurant partners, refresh day trip suggestions."
