## RESTAURANTS & DINING — FINGER LAKES EDITION

### 1. Stonecat Cafe (Fine Dining)
**Cuisine:** New American, farm-to-table
**Price:** $$$
**Distance:** 8 miles / 12 min drive
**Why we love it:** Stonecat is the reason we moved to the area. Chef's tasting menu changes weekly based on what the local farms are harvesting. The patio overlooks Seneca Lake at sunset — ask for table 4 if they have it.
**Order this:** The duck confit when it's on the menu, always the warm olive oil cake for dessert.
**Reservations:** Required, book two weeks ahead on weekends.
**Book:** [stonecatcafe.com](#) or (607) 555-0100
**Best for:** Anniversary dinner, milestone celebrations

### 2. Dano's Heuriger (Casual)
**Cuisine:** Austrian wine-country tavern
**Price:** $$
**Distance:** 6 miles / 10 min drive
**Why we love it:** Chef Dano built this place by hand. It's the kind of restaurant you tell people about at parties a month later. Small plates meant to pair with Finger Lakes Riesling. Warm, loud, joyful.
**Order this:** Jagerwurst, spaetzle, anything with sauerkraut.
**Reservations:** Recommended.
**Best for:** Couples, wine lovers

### 3. Nickel's BBQ (Casual Neighborhood)
**Cuisine:** Smoked BBQ, sides
**Price:** $$
**Distance:** 4 miles
**Why we love it:** No pretense. Picnic tables, paper plates, some of the best brisket north of the Mason-Dixon. Get there before 1 PM on weekends or they sell out.
**Order this:** Burnt ends sandwich, jalapeño cheddar grits.
**Best for:** Lunch, families

### 4. Glen Mountain Market (Quick Eats)
**Cuisine:** Deli, sandwiches, coffee
**Price:** $
**Distance:** 5 miles
**Why we love it:** Perfect grab-and-go before a Watkins Glen hike. Real sourdough bread, house-made pastrami.
**Order this:** The "Gorge" sandwich, iced chai.
**Best for:** Pre-hike breakfast, takeout

### 5. FLX Wienery (Late Night / Family)
**Cuisine:** Upscale hot dogs, fries, milkshakes
**Price:** $
**Distance:** 9 miles (Dundee)
**Why we love it:** Chef Christopher Bates (Master Sommelier) makes the best hot dog you will ever eat. Open late. Kids eat free on Tuesdays.
**Order this:** The "Trailer Trash" dog, truffle fries, black raspberry shake.
**Best for:** Kids, late dinner

## ACTIVITIES & ATTRACTIONS — FINGER LAKES

### 1. Watkins Glen State Park
**Type:** Outdoor — must-do
**Distance:** 3 miles
**Time needed:** 2–3 hours
**Cost:** $10 parking
**Why we love it:** 19 waterfalls in a single 1.5-mile gorge walk. Go at 8 AM to beat the crowds. The stone steps and bridges feel like a movie set — because they have been.
**Insider tip:** Park at the South Entrance lot, not the main one. Walk uphill first, coast downhill back to your car.

### 2. Corning Museum of Glass
**Type:** Cultural / rainy-day
**Distance:** 28 miles / 35 min drive
**Time needed:** Half day
**Cost:** $22 adults, kids under 17 free
**Why we love it:** World-class. Live glass-blowing demos every 30 minutes. Make-your-own-glass experience (book ahead — it sells out) is the single best souvenir you can bring home.
**Book:** [cmog.org](#)

### 3. Seneca Lake Kayaking (from our dock)
**Type:** Water sports
**Distance:** 0 miles — leave from the property
**Cost:** Included — two kayaks in the boathouse
**Why we love it:** The lake is glassy before 9 AM. Paddle south along the shoreline for 30 minutes and you'll see the waterfall at Hector Falls from the water — a view 99% of visitors never get.
**Insider tip:** Life jackets are in the boathouse on the left wall.

# SECTION 4: AFFILIATE REVENUE SETUP

## The Opportunity

The "Special Experiences" section of your guidebook is the single highest-ROI real estate in your entire rental business.

**The math:**

- Average Viator activity price: $50–$300
- Commission rate: 8%
- Commission per booking: $4–$24
- Typical guest conversion: 30% book at least one experience
- Average property bookings per year: 50–120

**Conservative estimate:** 50 stays × 30% conversion × $12 avg commission = **$180/yr**

**Realistic estimate:** 80 stays × 35% conversion × $18 avg commission = **$504/yr**

**Optimized estimate (premium property, curated list):** 100 stays × 40% conversion × $60 avg commission = **$2,400/yr**

This is passive. Once you set it up, it earns while you sleep.

## Step 1: Sign Up for Viator Affiliate Program

1. Go to [Viator Partner Program](https://www.viator.com/partner/) (viator.com/partner)
2. Apply as a "Content Publisher" (the host category)
3. Approval is usually 24–72 hours
4. Once approved, you get an affiliate dashboard + unique tracking ID

**Alternative programs to consider:**

- **GetYourGuide Partner Program** — similar 8% commission, better for European markets
- **TripAdvisor Experiences** — 5% commission, broader inventory
- **Airbnb Experiences** — no affiliate program yet, but worth listing your own if you're a qualified chef/guide
- **Local direct partnerships** — some boat charters and wineries will give you 10–15% if you negotiate directly

## Step 2: Find Activities in Your Market

1. In your Viator dashboard, search your city
2. Filter by "Top Rated" and 4.5+ stars only
3. Sort by commission rate (highest first)
4. Hand-pick 5–8 experiences that fit your ideal guest avatar

**Focus on these high-conversion categories:**

- Sunset boat tours (highest booking rate of any category)
- Wine/food tours with transportation included
- Private guides (high price point = high commission)
- Couples' photography sessions (gift-worthy, easy upsell)
- Helicopter / scenic flights (highest commission per booking)

## Step 3: Generate Affiliate Links

1. In the Viator dashboard, click on an activity
2. Click "Get Affiliate Link" → copy
3. Use a URL shortener (bit.ly or Notion's link embed) to clean it up

## Step 4: Place Links Naturally

**Do this:**

> *"Sarah and I went on the sunset lake cruise for our anniversary last year. The wine is mediocre but the golden hour on the water is unreal. [Book here → ](link)"*

**Don't do this:**

> *"Check out these 47 amazing activities in [your area]. BOOK NOW! [link] [link] [link]"*

The trust-to-conversion ratio is brutal. Guests can smell spam from a mile away. Five personal, vetted recommendations will outperform 47 generic ones every single time.

## Step 5: Track & Optimize

- Review your Viator dashboard monthly
- If an activity has 0 clicks in 60 days, swap it out
- If an activity converts well, feature it more prominently in the welcome page
- Test new experiences each season

## Pro Move: Stack Multiple Revenue Streams

Your guidebook can also include:

- **Amazon affiliate links** for gear you recommend (camping chairs, hiking boots, sunscreen)
- **Resy / OpenTable** restaurant referrals
- **Local business referral agreements** — direct deals with boat rental shops, spa services, etc.

# SECTION 5: SHAREABLE FORMATS & DELIVERY

## Method 1: Notion Public Link (Easiest)

**Setup time:** 30 seconds

1. On your Notion home page, click "Share" (top-right)
2. Toggle "Share to web" ON
3. Disable "Allow editing" (keep it read-only)
4. Copy the URL
5. Use a custom URL shortener for branding: `yourproperty.com/guide` redirects to the Notion page

**Pros:** Mobile-friendly, always up-to-date, free.
**Cons:** Requires internet at the property.

## Method 2: QR Code at Property (High-Impact)

**Setup time:** 10 minutes + Canva

1. Generate a QR code from your Notion URL at [qr-code-generator.com](https://www.qr-code-generator.com) (free)
2. Download as PNG
3. Open Canva → search "welcome card" → pick a template
4. Drop the QR code in, add text: "Scan for our local guide"
5. Print on thick cardstock (Moo.com or local print shop, $15 for 10)
6. Place one on the kitchen counter, one by the bed, one by the door

**Printable Card Layout (5" × 7"):**

```

│   {{PROPERTY NAME}}         │
│                             │
```
