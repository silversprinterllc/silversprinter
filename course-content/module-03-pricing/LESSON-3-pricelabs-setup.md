### PRICELABS SETUP (0:30 - 12:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"**[SCREEN RECORDING: pricelabs.co]**

**Step 1: Create your PriceLabs account ($19.99/month)**

Go to pricelabs.co. Click 'Start Free Trial' — you get 30 days free to test before paying.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Account creation]**

**Step 2: Connect your PMS or Airbnb account**

PriceLabs integrates with 100+ PMS platforms and with Airbnb directly.

If you use Hostaway, OwnerRez, Lodgify, or Guesty — connect through your PMS. This is the best path because rate changes push through the PMS to ALL your platforms simultaneously.

If you don't have a PMS yet, you can connect Airbnb directly.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Connecting PMS/Airbnb — showing integration page]**

Click your PMS name, authorize the connection, and PriceLabs will import your listing(s).

**Step 3: Review your market data**

Once connected, PriceLabs automatically pulls data for your market — comparable listings, seasonal demand, event calendar, and day-of-week patterns.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Market data dashboard in PriceLabs]**

This is similar to what we pulled from AirDNA — but PriceLabs uses this data in real-time to set your prices. You can see:
- Market occupancy trends
- Competitor rate ranges
- Demand forecast for the next 365 days
- Local events flagged automatically

**Step 4: Set your base rate**

Go to your listing's pricing page. Enter the base rate you calculated in Lesson 2.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Entering base rate]**

PriceLabs uses this as the starting point. All adjustments are relative to this base.

**Step 5: Set minimum and maximum rates**

Enter your floor (minimum) and ceiling (maximum) rates from Lesson 2.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Setting min/max]**

These are guardrails. No matter what demand looks like, PriceLabs will never go below your minimum or above your maximum. This protects you from extreme swings.

**Step 6: Configure seasonal adjustments**

PriceLabs auto-detects seasonal demand patterns, but you can fine-tune them. Based on your seasonal demand map from Lesson 2:

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Seasonal adjustment sliders]**

- Peak season: Set a positive adjustment (e.g., +20-40% above base)
- Shoulder season: Leave at base or slight positive (+5-10%)
- Low season: Set a negative adjustment (-10-20% below base)

Don't go too aggressive. Let PriceLabs' algorithm handle the micro-adjustments. Your seasonal settings just set the general range.

**Step 7: Configure day-of-week adjustments**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Day-of-week settings]**

Based on your day-of-week demand data:

- Friday: +10-20% above base
- Saturday: +15-25% above base
- Sunday: 0% to +5%
- Monday-Thursday: -5% to -15%

Again, these are starting points. PriceLabs will fine-tune daily based on real-time demand.

**Step 8: Set minimum night stays**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Minimum stay settings]**

Configure minimum night stays by season:

- Peak season: 3-5 night minimum (you don't want 1-night stays on July 4th weekend)
- Shoulder season: 2-3 night minimum
- Low season: 1-2 night minimum (fill the calendar)
- Events: 3-7 night minimum (depends on event duration)

PriceLabs can also set dynamic minimum stays that adjust based on how far out the booking is. For example: require 3 nights for bookings 30+ days out, but drop to 1 night for last-minute bookings within 7 days.

**Step 9: Set orphan day rules (GAP FILLING)**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Orphan day settings]**

Orphan days are the 1-2 day gaps between bookings that nobody books. They're the silent killer of occupancy.

PriceLabs can automatically:
- Detect gaps of 1-2 days between existing bookings
- Drop the minimum night stay to 1 for those specific days
- Reduce the nightly rate by 10-20% to incentivize filling the gap

Enable this. It fills nights that would otherwise go empty.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Enabling orphan day rules]**

**Step 10: Set last-minute discount rules**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Last-minute pricing settings]**

For nights that are still open within 3-7 days of check-in:

- 7 days out: -5% below current rate
- 3 days out: -10% below current rate
- 1 day out: -15% below current rate (if still empty)

This fills last-minute gaps without destroying your rate for advance bookings.

**Step 11: Set far-out booking rules (optional)**

For bookings more than 6 months in advance, you can set a slight premium (+5-10%). Guests who book far in advance are typically planning special trips and are less price-sensitive.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Far-out settings]**

**Step 12: Enable and sync**

Review all your settings. Hit 'Enable Dynamic Pricing.' PriceLabs will now push optimized rates to your PMS every day — which then syncs to Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and your direct site.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Enabling dynamic pricing, showing first rate push]**

Your prices are now dynamic. Tomorrow morning, every night on your calendar will have a data-driven rate. It adjusts daily based on market demand, competitor pricing, and your booking pace.

**Step 13: The PriceLabs Dashboard**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Dashboard tour]**

Check your dashboard weekly. It shows:
- Your recommended rates for the next 30/60/90 days
- Which dates are priced above or below your base
- Your occupancy forecast
- Revenue projections

If you disagree with a specific date's price, you can override it. But in my experience, the algorithm is right 90% of the time. Trust the data."
