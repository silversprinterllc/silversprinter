### LENGTH-OF-STAY DISCOUNTS (0:25 - 3:30) [CANON 70D + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Longer stays are worth more than they appear. Here's why:

**[SLIDE: The True Cost of Turnovers]**

Every turnover costs you:
- Cleaning: $100-$250 depending on property size
- Laundry: $20-$50 (if outsourced)
- Supplies restocking: $15-$30
- Vacant transition time: typically 4-6 hours of unbookable time
- Wear and tear: carpets, furniture, appliances get more use with more turnovers
- Your time: messaging, coordination, check-in/out management

**Total turnover cost: $150-$350 per turnover.**

Now compare:
- Four 2-night stays in 8 days = 4 turnovers = $600-$1,400 in costs
- One 7-night stay = 1 turnover = $150-$350 in costs

Even at a 15% weekly discount, the 7-night stay is often MORE profitable after turnover costs.

**[SLIDE: Discount Ladder]**

Here's the discount ladder I recommend:

| Stay Length | Discount | Logic |
|------------|---------|-------|
| 1-3 nights | 0% (base rate) | Short stays pay full price |
| 4-6 nights | 5% off | Small incentive for mid-week extension |
| 7-13 nights | 10-15% off | Weekly rate — saves you 3 turnovers |
| 14-27 nights | 18-22% off | Bi-weekly — minimal turnover, steady income |
| 28-89 nights | 25-35% off | Monthly rate — zero turnover, guaranteed income |
| 90+ nights | 35-45% off | Extended stay — essentially mid-term rental |

**[SCREEN RECORDING: PriceLabs length-of-stay discount settings]**

Let me configure this in PriceLabs. Go to your listing settings > Length-of-Stay Discounts.

Enter each tier:
- 7 nights: -12%
- 14 nights: -20%
- 28 nights: -30%

PriceLabs automatically applies the appropriate discount when a guest selects dates matching these thresholds.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Configuring discounts]**

You can also set these directly on Airbnb (they show as 'weekly discount' and 'monthly discount') and in your PMS for other platforms."

### FEE STRATEGY (3:30 - 6:30) [CANON 70D + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Beyond nightly rate, there are several fees that add revenue or protect your margins. Let's walk through each one.

**[SLIDE: Fee Inventory]**

**Cleaning Fee**

This is the most debated fee in STR. There are two schools of thought:

**Option A: Separate cleaning fee (transparent)**
- You charge a flat cleaning fee per stay ($100-$250 depending on property size)
- Pros: Guests understand they're paying for cleaning. It's visible in the booking total.
- Cons: High cleaning fees discourage short stays. Airbnb's algorithm slightly penalizes high total booking costs.

**Option B: Cleaning fee built into nightly rate (hidden)**
- You don't charge a separate cleaning fee. Instead, your nightly rate is slightly higher to cover it.
- Pros: Cleaner booking total. No sticker shock. Better for 1-2 night stays.
- Cons: Your nightly rate looks higher in search. Guests who stay 7+ nights are overpaying for cleaning relative to actual cost.

**My recommendation:** Use a moderate separate cleaning fee ($75-$150) for properties with 2+ night minimums. Build it into the rate ONLY if you allow 1-night stays.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Setting cleaning fee in PMS]**

**Pet Fee**

If you allow pets (and you should consider it — pet-friendly properties see 10-20% higher occupancy in many markets):
- Flat pet fee: $50-$100 per stay
- Or: $25-$50 per pet per night
- Set a maximum of 2 pets
- Require a pet deposit or pet damage waiver

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Pet fee configuration]**

**Extra Guest Fee**

If your property sleeps 8 but your base rate is priced for 6 guests:
- Charge $25-$50/night for each guest beyond 6
- This captures additional revenue from larger groups without raising your base rate

**Early Check-In / Late Check-Out**

Offer as an optional upsell:
- Early check-in (before standard time): $50-$100
- Late check-out (after standard time): $50-$100
- Only offer when there's no same-day turnover conflict

This is pure profit with zero additional cost."

### UPSELL REVENUE (6:30 - 9:00) [CANON 70D + SLIDES]

"Now let's talk about revenue that doesn't come from the nightly rate at all.

**[SLIDE: Upsell Menu]**

These are services you offer to guests — usually provided by someone else, where you earn a commission or markup.

**Airport Transfer / Transportation**
Partner with a local car service. Offer airport pickup/dropoff at $75-$150. Your cut: 20-30%.

**Grocery Stocking**
Offer to stock the fridge before arrival. Guest pays $100-$200 for groceries + $50 service fee. You place the order at Instacart or partner with a local service. Your profit: the $50 service fee.

**Experience Packages**
We cover this in depth in Module 9, but the quick version: partner with a local personal chef ($75-$150/person), wine tour operator, boat charter, or surf instructor. Offer these as add-ons during the pre-arrival email. Your commission: 10-20%.

**Mid-Stay Cleaning**
For stays of 7+ nights, offer a mid-stay cleaning at $75-$125. Your cleaning crew does it. You charge the guest directly. Margin: 30-50%.

**[SLIDE: The Upsell Email (Pre-Arrival)]**

The best time to offer upsells is in your pre-arrival email (3-5 days before check-in). Here's the template:

Subject: 'Make Your [City] Trip Even Better'

Body:
'Hi [Name]! We're getting [Property Name] ready for your arrival on [Date]. Want to make your trip even more special? Here are some guest favorites:

- Airport pickup in a private car: $[X] → [Book link]
- Fridge stocked with groceries on arrival: $[X] → [Book link]
- Private chef dinner for [X] guests: $[X]/person → [Book link]
- [Local experience] with [partner]: $[X] → [Book link]

Just reply to this email if you'd like to add any of these!

See you soon,
[Your name]'

**[SLIDE: Revenue Impact]**

If 20% of guests purchase one $100 upsell, and you have 200 guests per year:
- 40 upsells × $100 = $4,000 gross
- Your margin (20-50%): $800-$2,000/year additional revenue

That's revenue from services you DON'T personally provide. Pure leverage."
