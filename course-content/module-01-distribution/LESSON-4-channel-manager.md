## VIDEO SCRIPT

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:30) [CANON 70D]

"You now have listings on 4 platforms. Here's the problem — if someone books July 4th weekend on VRBO while those dates are also available on Airbnb and Booking.com, you just got triple-booked. And you're about to ruin three guests' vacations and collect three one-star reviews.

A channel manager prevents this. It syncs your calendar across every platform in real-time. One booking on VRBO instantly blocks those dates on Airbnb, Booking.com, Google, and your direct site. This lesson — set it up and never worry about double bookings again."

### WHAT A CHANNEL MANAGER DOES (0:30 - 2:00) [OBSBOT + SLIDES]

"A channel manager is the central nervous system of your distribution strategy. It does three things:

**[SLIDE: 3 Functions]**

**1. Calendar Sync**
When a booking comes in on any platform, the channel manager instantly blocks those dates on every other platform. When a cancellation happens, those dates reopen everywhere. Real-time, automatic, no manual work.

**2. Rate Sync**
Set your base rate in one place and push it to all platforms — with platform-specific adjustments. Remember, we set VRBO +5-8% and Booking.com +5-10%. The channel manager handles this math automatically.

**3. Unified Inbox**
Guest messages from Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and your direct site all land in one inbox. No switching between 4 apps. One place to manage all communication.

**[SLIDE: Before vs After diagram]**

Before channel manager: You're logging into 4 platforms daily, manually blocking dates, manually checking messages, living in fear of double bookings.

After channel manager: Everything syncs automatically. You check one dashboard. You respond to one inbox. You sleep at night."

### CHOOSING YOUR CHANNEL MANAGER (2:00 - 4:00) [CANON 70D]

"There are two paths depending on your situation:

**[SLIDE: Two Paths]**

**Path A: Your PMS has a built-in channel manager.**

If you're using Hostaway, Guesty, Lodgify, or OwnerRez — congratulations, your channel manager is already included. These PMS platforms have native integrations with Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com, and Google. You just need to connect them.

This is the path I recommend for most hosts. Your PMS and channel manager should be the same tool — fewer things to break.

**Path B: Standalone channel manager.**

If you're using a simpler PMS that doesn't include channel management (like Hospitable for 1-2 properties), you can add a standalone channel manager like:
- iCal sync (free but slow — updates every 1-4 hours, NOT real-time)
- Rentals United
- NextPax

I strongly recommend Path A. The $40-100/month you spend on a PMS with built-in channel management saves you from the double-booking nightmare that WILL happen with free iCal syncing. It's not a question of if — it's when.

**[SLIDE: PMS Recommendation by Portfolio Size]**

| Properties | Recommended PMS | Monthly Cost | Channel Manager |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 1-3 units | Hospitable or OwnerRez | $29-$60/mo | Built-in (OwnerRez) or iCal (Hospitable) |
| 3-10 units | Hostaway or Lodgify | $60-$200/mo | Built-in, real-time |
| 10+ units | Hostaway or Guesty | $200+/mo | Built-in, enterprise-grade |

If you haven't chosen a PMS yet, we cover this in detail in Module 4 (Automation). For now, I'm going to walk through the setup using Hostaway as the example — but the process is similar across all major PMS platforms."

### SETUP WALKTHROUGH (4:00 - 9:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Let's connect everything.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Hostaway dashboard]**

**Step 1: Add your property to the PMS.**

In Hostaway (or your PMS), create your property listing with all the basics — address, bedrooms, bathrooms, photos, description. This becomes your 'master listing' that feeds to all platforms.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Creating property in PMS]**

**Step 2: Connect Airbnb.**

Go to Channels > Airbnb > Connect. Hostaway will ask you to authorize the connection through your Airbnb account. Once connected, your Airbnb calendar, messages, and reservations sync automatically.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Airbnb connection flow]**

Important: when you connect Airbnb to your PMS, the PMS becomes the master. Edit your listing in the PMS, and changes push to Airbnb. If you edit directly on Airbnb, it may get overwritten. Get in the habit of managing everything through the PMS from this point forward.

**Step 3: Connect VRBO.**

Same process — Channels > VRBO > Connect. VRBO uses an API connection with most major PMS platforms. Authorize, sync, done.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: VRBO connection flow]**

**Step 4: Connect Booking.com.**

Booking.com requires a slightly different process. You may need to request API access through their Extranet or use your PMS's Booking.com integration form. Hostaway handles this automatically — you enter your Booking.com property ID and it connects.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Booking.com connection]**

Note: Booking.com connections can take 24-48 hours to fully activate. Be patient.

**Step 5: Set platform-specific rate adjustments.**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Rate adjustment settings]**

In your PMS, find the rate management section. Set your base rate and then set multipliers per platform:

- Airbnb: Base rate (1.0x)
- VRBO: 1.05x to 1.08x (covers lower commission)
- Booking.com: 1.05x to 1.10x (covers higher commission)
- Direct booking site: 0.95x (give guests an incentive to book direct)

The PMS automatically calculates the actual nightly rate for each platform.

**Step 6: Test the sync.**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Creating a test block]**

Block 3 dates on your PMS calendar. Then check each platform — within minutes, those dates should be blocked on Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com.

Unblock them. Check again. Dates should reopen everywhere.

If something doesn't sync, check your connection status in the PMS dashboard. Common issues: expired API tokens (re-authorize), Booking.com pending activation, or VRBO rate plan not mapped.

**Step 7: Set up the unified inbox.**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Unified inbox]**

Go to your PMS inbox. You should now see messages from all connected platforms in one view. Each message is tagged with the platform it came from (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com).

Reply from here — your response goes back to the guest on their platform. One inbox. All platforms."
