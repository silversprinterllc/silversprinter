### HOW THE ALGORITHM WORKS (0:40 - 3:00) [OBSBOT + SLIDES]

"Airbnb's search algorithm is a matching system. When a guest searches for a property in your area, the algorithm scores every listing and ranks them. The highest-scoring listings show up first.

**[SLIDE: 'The 7 Ranking Factors']**

The algorithm weighs these factors, roughly in this order of importance:

**1. Search Relevance**
Does your listing match what the guest is searching for? If they search for a 3-bedroom in Destin with a pool, and you have a 3-bedroom in Destin with a pool — you're in the running. If your listing title and description don't mention 'pool' even though you have one, the algorithm may not surface you.

This is listing SEO — and most hosts are terrible at it. We'll fix yours in a few minutes.

**2. Booking Probability**
The algorithm predicts how likely THIS specific guest is to book YOUR listing. It looks at the guest's past booking behavior, their saved listings, their search patterns, and matches that against your listing's characteristics. You can't directly control this, but you CAN increase it by having a well-rounded listing that appeals broadly.

**3. Response Rate and Speed**
How quickly do you respond to inquiries and booking requests? The algorithm heavily penalizes slow responders. Target: respond within 1 hour. Ideally within 15 minutes.

If you're thinking 'I can't check Airbnb every 15 minutes' — that's exactly why we automate messaging in Module 4.

**4. Booking Rate (Conversion)**
Of the people who VIEW your listing, what percentage actually BOOK? A high view-to-book ratio tells the algorithm your listing is compelling. Low conversion means something is turning guests off — price, photos, description, reviews, or cancellation policy.

**5. Review Quality and Quantity**
More five-star reviews = higher ranking. But it's not just the number — the algorithm also weighs recency. A listing with 10 reviews in the last 3 months outranks a listing with 50 reviews but none in the last 6 months.

We build a review generation engine in Module 7 that automates this entirely.

**6. Pricing Competitiveness**
Is your nightly rate in line with comparable properties? The algorithm doesn't reward the cheapest listing — it rewards the best VALUE. Underpricing can actually hurt you because it signals low quality. Overpricing hurts you because guests don't book.

Module 3 (Dynamic Pricing) solves this permanently with real-time data.

**7. Guest Favorite Status**
In 2024, Airbnb replaced Superhost with the Guest Favorite program. Guest Favorite badges are awarded to the top 2% of listings based on reviews, ratings, and reliability. If you earn it, you get a badge, priority search placement, and higher booking rates."

### GUEST FAVORITE PROGRAM (3:00 - 4:30) [CANON 70D]

"Let's talk about Guest Favorite because this is the new gold standard.

**[SLIDE: Guest Favorite Requirements]**

To earn Guest Favorite status, Airbnb evaluates:

- **Overall rating:** 4.9 or higher
- **Individual category ratings:** High scores in cleanliness, accuracy, check-in, communication, location, and value
- **Review volume:** Enough reviews to establish a pattern (typically 10+)
- **Reliability:** Low cancellation rate, fast response time, no major issues
- **Guest sentiment:** Airbnb's AI actually reads your review TEXT — not just the star rating. Positive language patterns boost your score.

The Guest Favorite badge appears prominently on your listing. Properties with the badge see 10-20% higher booking rates than identical properties without it.

How to get it:
- Maintain a 4.9+ average (we teach you how to handle the occasional 4-star in Module 7)
- Respond to every inquiry within 1 hour
- Never cancel on guests (Airbnb penalizes host cancellations severely)
- Ensure your listing is 100% accurate — the #1 cause of bad reviews is 'not as described'
- Go above and beyond on cleanliness — this is the category guests rate most harshly"

### LISTING SEO OPTIMIZATION (4:30 - 8:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Now let's optimize your listing for the algorithm. Open your Airbnb listing editor — I'll walk you through each section.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Airbnb listing editor]**

**Title (80 characters max)**

Your title is the first thing guests see in search results AND it's what the algorithm uses for keyword matching.

Bad title: 'Beautiful Home Near Beach'
Better title: 'Oceanview 3BR Retreat | Private Pool | Steps to Beach | Sleeps 8'

The formula:

**[Property Feature] + [Bedrooms/Type] + [Top Amenity] + [Location Detail] + [Guest Count]**

Pack in keywords that guests actually search for. People search 'pool,' 'ocean view,' 'pet friendly,' 'hot tub,' 'walk to beach.' If you have it, PUT IT IN THE TITLE.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Editing a title live]**

**Description (First 2 lines are critical)**

Airbnb shows only the first 2 lines of your description before 'Show more.' Those two lines must hook the guest — just like the first 2 seconds of a TikTok.

Bad first line: 'Welcome to our beautiful home!'
Better first line: 'Wake up to ocean views from your private balcony, walk 2 minutes to the sand, and come home to a heated pool and outdoor kitchen.'

Lead with the EXPERIENCE, not the welcome mat.

After the hook, structure your description like this:

1. The experience (what it FEELS like to stay here — 2-3 sentences)
2. The space (bedrooms, bathrooms, unique features — bullet format)
3. The location (what's nearby, walking distance to attractions)
4. The amenities (everything noteworthy — hot tub, game room, grill, etc.)
5. The details (check-in/out times, house rules, parking)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Walking through a well-structured description]**

**Photos (Most important conversion factor)**

The algorithm tracks how long guests look at your photos. More time on photos = higher engagement signal = higher ranking.

Photo sequence that converts:
1. HERO SHOT — your single best photo. Usually the exterior or the view. This is what shows in search results.
2. Living room — wide angle showing space and design
3. Kitchen — clean, bright, shows appliances
4. Master bedroom — made bed, good lighting
5. Best amenity — pool, hot tub, view, game room
6. Bathroom — clean, bright
7. Outdoor space — deck, patio, yard
8. Location context — beach, mountains, downtown
9-15. Additional rooms and details

DO NOT use iPhone snapshots. Professional photos are the single highest-ROI investment in your listing. $200-$500 once will pay for itself in higher bookings within the first month.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Photo reordering in Airbnb editor]**

**Amenities Checklist**

Go through Airbnb's full amenity checklist and check EVERYTHING that applies. Guests filter by amenities (pool, WiFi, washer, kitchen, parking, etc.) — if you have it but haven't checked the box, you're invisible in filtered searches.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Scrolling through amenities checklist]**

Common ones hosts miss: dedicated workspace, self check-in, free parking, EV charger, outdoor shower, fire pit, board games, beach gear."

### QUICK WINS CHECKLIST (10:00 - 11:00) [CANON 70D]

"Let me give you the quick wins — things you can do TODAY that will improve your ranking within a week.

**[SLIDE: Quick Wins Checklist]**

1. Rewrite your title using the formula: Feature + Type + Amenity + Location + Guests
2. Rewrite your first 2 description lines — lead with experience, not 'welcome'
3. Reorder your photos — hero shot first, best amenity by photo 5
4. Complete the full amenity checklist — check every box that applies
5. Turn on Instant Book if you haven't already — the algorithm rewards it
6. Set a competitive cancellation policy — flexible or moderate (not strict)
7. Respond to your last 5 messages within the app to boost response rate metrics
8. Lower your minimum night stay by one night (if you're at 3, try 2) — increases booking eligible searches

These eight changes take about 60-90 minutes. Do them today."
