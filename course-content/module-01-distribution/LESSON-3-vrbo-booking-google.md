### VRBO SETUP (0:30 - 4:30) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Let's start with VRBO. This is your highest-priority add because of the family demographic and the lower commission rate.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: vrbo.com owner signup]**

**Step 1: Create your owner account.**

Go to vrbo.com and click 'List Your Property.' You'll need:
- Email address (use a different one than your Airbnb if possible — keeps accounts separate)
- Phone number
- Property address
- Property type (entire home, condo, cabin, etc.)

**Step 2: Property details.**

VRBO asks for bedrooms, bathrooms, max guests, and amenities. Fill in everything — same principle as Airbnb, check every box that applies.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Filling in property details]**

**Step 3: Photos.**

Upload the same professional photos you use on Airbnb BUT in a different order. VRBO guests are primarily families — lead with:
1. Full exterior shot showing the whole property
2. Living/family room — spacious, inviting
3. Kitchen — families cook, show the full kitchen
4. Kids' room or bunk room (if applicable)
5. Outdoor space — yard, pool, deck
6. Master bedroom
7. Bathrooms

Families want to see SPACE. They want to visualize their kids running around. Adjust your photo sequence accordingly.

**Step 4: Title and description.**

DON'T copy-paste your Airbnb listing. VRBO guests respond to different language.

Airbnb title might be: 'Sun-Drenched Coastal Retreat | Steps to Beach | Sleeps 8'
VRBO title should be: 'Family-Friendly Beach House | Private Pool | Steps to Sand | 3BR Sleeps 8'

Notice: 'Family-friendly' is in the VRBO title because that's who's searching there. Tailor to the platform.

For the description, lead with what families care about:
- Safety features (fenced yard, no steep stairs, baby gear available)
- Space for everyone (multiple living areas, large dining table)
- Entertainment (game room, pool, beach gear, board games)
- Convenience (full kitchen, washer/dryer, parking for 2 cars)
- Nearby kid-friendly activities

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Writing a VRBO-optimized description]**

**Step 5: Pricing and availability.**

Set your nightly rate slightly HIGHER than Airbnb — VRBO's commission is lower (5-8% vs 15%), so you can charge more and the guest pays roughly the same total. I recommend adding 5-8% to your Airbnb rate on VRBO.

Set your calendar to manually managed for now. We'll connect the channel manager in Lesson 4 to auto-sync.

**Step 6: Publish.**

Review everything, click publish. Your VRBO listing is now live.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Clicking publish]**

VRBO takes 24-72 hours to fully index your listing in search. Don't panic if you don't see bookings immediately."

### BOOKING.COM SETUP (4:30 - 8:30) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Now let's add Booking.com. This is your international and last-minute booking channel.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Booking.com partner registration]**

**Step 1: Register as a partner.**

Go to join.booking.com. Select 'Homes and Apartments.' You'll need:
- Legal name and contact info
- Property address
- Government ID (Booking.com requires identity verification)
- Tax information

Booking.com's signup process is more thorough than Airbnb or VRBO. It can take 3-7 days for full account approval. Start this today even if you're not ready to list — the clock starts when you apply.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Registration form]**

**Step 2: Property setup in the Extranet.**

Booking.com's host dashboard is called the 'Extranet.' It's more complex than Airbnb's interface but more powerful.

Key sections to complete:
- Property layout (rooms, beds, bathrooms — be VERY specific, Booking.com guests expect accuracy)
- Facilities and services (amenities — same principle, check everything)
- Photos (same professional photos, but Booking.com allows more categories — upload to specific room types)
- Policies (cancellation, check-in/out, house rules)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Navigating the Extranet, filling in sections]**

**Step 3: Listing content.**

Booking.com descriptions work differently — they're more factual and amenity-focused than Airbnb. International travelers want clarity, not poetry.

Bad: 'Experience the magic of coastal living in our sun-kissed retreat...'
Better: '3-bedroom beach house with private pool, 200m from the beach. Free WiFi, full kitchen, washer/dryer, free parking for 2 vehicles. Air conditioning in all rooms.'

Short sentences. Specific distances. Amenity-first. This is what converts on Booking.com.

**Step 4: Pricing.**

Booking.com charges 15% commission. Factor this into your rate. I recommend setting your Booking.com rate 5-10% HIGHER than your Airbnb rate to offset the commission difference.

You can also enable 'Genius' discounts — a loyalty program where Booking.com's frequent travelers get 10-15% off. This sounds expensive but Genius travelers book more, cancel less, and leave better reviews. Consider enabling it after your first 5-10 bookings.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Setting up rates]**

**Step 5: Availability.**

Set to manually managed for now. We'll connect the channel manager in Lesson 4.

IMPORTANT: Until your channel manager is connected, you MUST manually block dates on Booking.com that are booked on Airbnb/VRBO. Double bookings on Booking.com result in penalties and can suspend your listing."

### GOOGLE VACATION RENTALS (8:30 - 11:00) [OBSBOT + SCREEN RECORDING]

"Now the free one — Google Vacation Rentals.

There are two ways to get on Google Vacation Rentals:

**Option A: Through your PMS (easiest)**

If you use Lodgify, Hostaway, OwnerRez, or certain other PMS platforms, they automatically feed your listing to Google. You just need to enable the Google integration in your PMS settings.

**[SCREEN RECORDING: PMS Google integration toggle]**

This is the simplest path. If you're already on one of these platforms (which we set up in Module 4), it's a checkbox.

**Option B: Through your direct booking website**

If you have a direct booking website (which we build in Module 2), you can submit your property to Google through their vacation rental partner program or through structured data markup on your site.

Lodgify and OwnerRez handle this automatically when they build your direct booking site.

**Either way — here's what to optimize on Google:**

**Google Business Profile**

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Google Business Profile setup]**

Whether or not you're on Google Vacation Rentals specifically, you NEED a Google Business Profile for your property. This is how you show up in Google Maps and local search.

Step 1: Go to business.google.com
Step 2: Create a new business profile
Step 3: Category: select 'Vacation rental' or 'Holiday rental'
Step 4: Add your property address, phone, website (your direct booking URL)
Step 5: Add photos (same professional photos)
Step 6: Write a business description — keyword-rich, mention your city, property type, and top amenities
Step 7: Verify your listing (Google sends a postcard or calls to verify)

**[SCREEN RECORDING: Completing Google Business Profile]**

Once verified, your property appears on Google Maps when travelers search for vacation rentals in your area. This drives both Google Vacation Rental bookings AND direct traffic to your website.

The ROI is infinite — it costs nothing and drives bookings forever."

See you there."
